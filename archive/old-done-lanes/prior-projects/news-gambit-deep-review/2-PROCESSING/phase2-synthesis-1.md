## S3. The produce wave

**(a) Name + thesis:** Two concurrently running, grower-traceable produce outbreaks in one season — published as scattered recall notices — are one import-verification failure, not two accidents.

**(b) Verified evidence:**
- A1 ✅ VERIFIED — Salmonella Javiana from jalapeños grown in Sinaloa, Mexico, distributed by Coast Citrus: 431 cases (86 new in latest update), 32 states, 57 hospitalizations, 0 deaths (FDA outbreak page; CDC fast-facts; CIDRAP).
