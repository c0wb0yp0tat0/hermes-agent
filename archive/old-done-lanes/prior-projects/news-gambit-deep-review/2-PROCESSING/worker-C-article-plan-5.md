# Worker C — Article Plan, Chunk 5: Verification Gate + Two-Way Doors

## Verification gate — what must be re-checked against primary sources BEFORE any prose is drafted

The published essay's authority rests on "Confidence: High — [source] primary." The sequel inherits the same obligation. These are the load-bearing numbers and the source each must be re-confirmed against before writing:

1. **18 of 20 Fortune 500 companies** with North Korean IT workers (Part 1 #1) → ITRC H1 2026 report + FBI NK-IT advisory + 11-country joint advisory (Jul 31, 2026). This is the Act I headline; if the "18 of 20" is a single threat-hunter's sampling (as the compile states), the essay must attribute it as such.
2. **21 malicious-insider events in H1 2026 vs 3 in all of 2025** (#2) → ITRC H1 2026. Also 1,803 breaches / 471M notices / $893.3M AI-fraud (FBI IC3).
3. **~20% of global seafood mislabeled, 38.1% at retail** (#7) → FAO Technical Paper 742.
4. **420 recalls / ~40M items / 12 deaths** (#12) → U.S. PIRG "Safe At Home in 2026" + CPSC database.
5. **431 cases / 32 states (jalapeño)** and **55 cases / 15 states, two pathogens (sprouts)** (A1, A2) → CDC outbreak pages (8/21/26 and 8/24/26) + FDA. Verify the counts have not moved since the compile's Aug 21–24 dates.
6. **90% of biomedical papers show signs of AI help** (#13) → Nature coverage; **mandatory**: re-read the underlying methodology before repeating the figure — the compile itself flags the definitional problem ("detector-based, self-reported, or style-based") and rates this Medium. This is the single most fragile number in the plan.
7. **PJM residential bills +44%, capacity $29 → $329/MW-day** (#3) → PJM + Sightline Climate.
8. **92,000 dams / $165.2B / 18% high-hazard** (#9) → Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists (5/11/26), ASCE, ASDSO.
9. **509,926-person Medicare analysis, 24% dementia reduction** (A3) and **495,037-cell hippocampus map** (A4) → *Annals of Internal Medicine* and *Nature Medicine* respectively; A3's small vaccinated group (8,843) and GSK funding must appear in the prose.
10. **A5 preprint status** (not peer-reviewed, transient effect) and **A8 interim status** (no survival data) → must be stated as such; these two carry the essay's honesty.

Rule: if any figure cannot be re-confirmed in its primary source, the section is rewritten around the confirmed remainder — the essay never publishes a number it cannot source.

## Two-way doors — the author's open decisions (any combination is safe)

1. **Hermes/AI section (Part 2 Section C)?** Default: **out** — stream separation rules keep Hermes news out of the general pool, and the essay's reader cannot be assumed to care about the author's own product. The one defensible inclusion: the meta-shape ("a claim that only holds under idle assumptions") as a single Act III sentence, unattributed to Hermes.
2. **Astrobiology?** Default: **out** — already told in the published essay. Only if the author wants a third science paragraph, use the 3I/ATLAS methane or organizational-biosignature items, clearly fresh-angled.
3. **#6 social media harm?** Default: **out of body**, reserve the Mosseri testimony / Brazil fine (B6) as a footnote or a single closing-act mention if the author wants the regulatory thread.
4. **Length?** The published essay is ~19 min read. Recommended sequel: **similar or shorter** (12–16 min) — one big story in four acts, not ten mini-essays.
5. **One big story vs several?** Recommended: **one big story** — the verification-collapse thesis across all four acts, with the fresh-science act as the counterpoint, exactly as the published essay is one big story with one thesis.
6. **Title?** "The Verification Is Missing You" (resemblance) vs "They Didn't Break In; They Got Hired" (the quote). Author's call.
