# Worker C — Article Plan, Chunk 2: Structure Map

## Architecture (mirrors the published essay)

1. **Opening move** — a variant of the published lead ("Let me be clear about what you are not being told…"). New version targets the verification layer: "Let me be clear about what nobody is checking. Not what the papers spin — what the systems themselves let through, because each check looked like a one-off, because a hired attacker is not a breach until you count the hires." Preserve the contract line verbatim in spirit: get the facts straight first, then misconstrue. End the open with the thesis sentence: the weakest point is the check, and the check is missing you on purpose.

2. **Section order & escalation** — the published essay escalates from public-health (Cyclospora) through infrastructure (water PLCs), supply chains (recalls, forced labor), economics (water bankruptcy), to science, then lands the thesis. The sequel escalates on the trust/verification axis, from concrete attacks to systemic collapse:
   - **Act I — the perimeter lied** (identity & hiring): laptop farm (#1), trust-economy insider surge (#2), healthcare wave (A7).
   - **Act II — the goods are unverified** (supply chains & recalls): seafood fraud (#7), forced labor in critical minerals (#8), 420 recalls (#12), produce outbreaks (A1, A2).
   - **Act III — the checks themselves are failing** (systems & science): AI power/water walls (#3), critical-minerals chokepoint (#4), 90% AI-help literature (#13 + B13 update), aging dams (#9).
   - **Act IV — the new floor** (fresh science as the counter-evidence that checking still works): quantum sunlight (#10), 100M-person stroke study (#11), shingles/dementia (A3), hippocampus map (A4), mitochondria eye (A5), little red dots (A6), mRNA vaccine (A8).
   - **Thesis landing** — the frame is the news; the verification layer is the new firewall; the checks that remain are the ones that cannot be photographed.

3. **Escalation device** — each act ends on the published essay's own move: "the pattern is the story," stated with fresh numbers so the escalation is felt (from 1,803 breaches (Part 1 #2) to 420 recalls (Part 1 #12) to 90% of papers (Part 1 #13)).

4. **Where the thesis lands** — the closing thesis paragraph is a near-callback to the published close: not under-informed by accident, but under-verified by format. "The news is missing you. The checks are missing the pattern. Read the primary sources. The floor holds." The synthesis note closes the piece (as in the published essay's "Synthesis of the 2026-08-21 misc-news gambit" note) with the compile's own count.
