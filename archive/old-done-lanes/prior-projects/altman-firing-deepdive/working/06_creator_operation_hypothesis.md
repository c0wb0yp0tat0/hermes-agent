**Key analytical frame — pre-bunked malinformation:** The user introduced the operative concept. Definition as applied here:
- **Malinformation** = content that is *factually true* but framed/contextualized to mislead or manipulate.
- **Pre-bunking** = forewarning an audience of a manipulation *technique* (the frame/method), so they resist it — vs. fact-checking after the fact.
- **Pre-bunked malinformation** (user's usage) = material where **the underlying numbers/events are correct, but the framing, selection, or visual arrangement carries a load the facts don't support** — making it resistant to normal "debunking" (because each isolated fact checks out). The distortion lives in the *framing*, not the data.
- This is the exact risk the user flagged on the charts: **"the numbers may be right but framing is critical."** The investigation's job is to locate where framing does work the facts don't justify — not to pronounce true/false.
