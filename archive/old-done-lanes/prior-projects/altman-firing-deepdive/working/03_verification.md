## 3.12 Addendum 2 — full-text New Yorker review (closed the real gap)

The `sources/` copy of the New Yorker was truncated (head+tail). The full 114 KB page in `/root/.hermes/cache/web/` was read end-to-end. This **upgrades two verdicts and adds an objectivity note**:
