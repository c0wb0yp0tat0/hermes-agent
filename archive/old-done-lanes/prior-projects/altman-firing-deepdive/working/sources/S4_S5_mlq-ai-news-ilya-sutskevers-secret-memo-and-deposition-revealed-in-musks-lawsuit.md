# Ilya Sutskever’s Secret Memo and Deposition Revealed in Musk’s Lawsuit Against OpenAI

Nov 4, 2025·2:12 PM·by MLQ Agent·2 min read

Key points

- Ilya Sutskever, former OpenAI Chief Scientist, confirmed under oath the existence of a secret 52-page memo critical of Sam Altman’s leadership, sparking Altman’s brief removal in November 2023[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).
- The memo was prepared at the request of an independent board member and distributed exclusively to three independent directors, not to Altman, who was kept in the dark[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).
- Sutskever said he feared Altman would suppress the memo if he became aware of it, so he used a disappearing email to send it[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).
- These revelations come from a newly surfaced 10-hour deposition transcript from Elon Musk’s ongoing lawsuit against OpenAI, centering on governance concerns and a potential merger with Anthropic[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic)[\[3\]](https://www.rundown.ai/articles/the-memos-behind-altmans-ousting).
- The deposition also reveals internal divisions and a serious, year-long consideration among some board members to remove Altman[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).

![Ilya Sutskever’s Secret Memo and Deposition Revealed in Musk’s Lawsuit Against OpenAI](https://mlq.ai/media/news_images/elon-ilya.png)

Newly released court documents from Elon Musk’s lawsuit against OpenAI have brought to light a secret 52-page memo written by Ilya Sutskever—OpenAI’s former Chief Scientist—that catalogs concerns about Sam Altman’s leadership and played a key role in Altman’s abrupt removal as CEO in November 2023[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). The memo was only shared with independent board members, who acted without informing Altman, according to Sutskever’s deposition testimony[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).

## The Memo’s Genesis and Distribution

Sutskever testified that the memo was commissioned by independent board member Adam D’Angelo and compiled from secondhand allegations about Altman’s management style[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). Sutskever sent the document—which has achieved near-mythical status among observers—exclusively to the board’s three independent directors: Adam D’Angelo, Helen Toner, and Tasha McCauley, using a "disappearing email" to reduce the risk of leaks[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). He intentionally withheld it from Altman, fearing the discussion would be suppressed if Altman learned of the document[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). The memo’s preparation and eventual use as a pretext for Altman’s ouster were the culmination of nearly a year of deliberation among certain board members[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).

## Context of Musk’s Lawsuit

The deposition, which lasted nearly 10 hours and was taken on October 1, 2025, is part of Elon Musk’s broader legal challenge to OpenAI’s transformation into a for-profit entity and its governance practices[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic)[\[3\]](https://www.rundown.ai/articles/the-memos-behind-altmans-ousting). Musk’s lawsuit seeks to clarify the organization’s original nonprofit mission and questions the legitimacy of Altman’s leadership and the board’s decision-making process[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). The deposition also reveals that some board members considered a merger with rival Anthropic as a contingency measure during the crisis[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic).

## The 72-Hour Crisis and Aftermath

The memo’s existence and contents became pivotal during the 72-hour period in November 2023 when Altman was temporarily removed as CEO before quickly being reinstated following widespread employee and investor backlash[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). Sutskever’s testimony explains the secrecy surrounding the coup attempt and the internal divisions that led to the board’s swift, but short-lived, action[\[1\]](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/ilya-sutskevers-secret-memo-and-the-plot-to-merge-openai-with-anthropic). The episode remains a focal point in ongoing discussions about corporate governance and transparency in AI organizations.
