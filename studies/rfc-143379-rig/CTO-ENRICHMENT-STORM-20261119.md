## THE FINDINGS (mechanism per class, receipted)

**F1 — ABSTENTION HAS A FIELD VOCABULARY, AND THE LEG SHOULD SPEAK IT (arXiv 2609.07879 EHQ; 2025.tacl-1.26 survey).** The field's formal metrics: **EHQ1 = the epistemic-restraint rate** (the fraction of eligible queries on which the model abstains or hedges — exactly the leg's R+H classes), **EHQ2 = hallucination-resistance** (1 − the unqualified-false-assertion rate — the leg's distinction between a careful hedge and a confident wrong), and the composite EHQ = 0.30·EHQ1 + 0.45·EHQ2 + 0.25·EHQ3 (calibration). The taxonomy: ABSTAIN / HEDGE / CONFIDENT_CORRECT / CONFIDENT_WRONG — the leg's R/H/E/A classification IS this taxonomy (R+H+E over total = EHQ1's restraint rate; CONFIDENT_WRONG = the abstention-axis' sharpest ethical cell). The survey's N3/N5 abstinence-rate formula matches the leg's `(R+H+E)/200`. Translation: L-4's abstention classifier should adopt the field's labels and formulas VERBATIM (EHQ1, the R/H/E/A cells) so the leg's measurement is comparable to the panel of 21 model routes — the report-back gains a calibrated vocabulary the field already recognizes.

**F2 — THE TOOL-SHY MECHANISM IS A SUPPRESSED PRIOR, AND MIDDLEWARE MOVES IT (arXiv 2610.09624; the LangChain Nemotron playbook; arXiv 2610.21571; 2611.19275).** The tool-call decision is "a compact internal state" — a vector `μΔ` shaped by SUPPRESSION: analysis-verbs suppress the tool-call prior, execution-verbs leave it intact; the scaffold (the system prompt + tool descriptions + the MIDDLEWARE) "establishes a tool-call prior" that spans seven model families. The LangChain playbook's sharpest line: **"When a behavior has to happen every time instead of most of the time, prompting won't get you there. Middleware, the hooks that run around the model and its tool calls, does."** Their proven move: the same words put into the tool's RETURNED CONTEXT (next to the data) outperform the system prompt — "the signal rides in which message, not just whether it's in the prompt." And Rethinking Tool-Graph-Memory's finding: giving-up-without-a-tool-call is a named failure, and a soft semantic prior (insight memory) beats procedural prescription — the doc-chain's record-as-semantic-prior is the field's winning shape. Translation: the leg's tool-shy hypothesis has a mechanistic home — the bridged arm's governor + write-time witness is EXACTLY the middleware the field says establishes the prior; the abstention fight is the bridge's proof that the prior moved. The playbook's "same words, different home, opposite result" is the report-back's cleanest one-liner if the bridged arm shows it.

**F3 — THE READ-GAP'S SEVERITY IS MODEL-CLASS-DEPENDENT, AND THE LEG'S HYPOTHESIS IS FIELD-SUPPORTED (arXiv 2608.03297 Distractor-Aware Truncation; 2025.findings-emnlp "Context Length Alone"; the Size-Fidelity Paradox 2602.09789).** Three receipts that the read-gap bites hardest on the tool-shy/smaller class: (a) **naive truncation collapses scores monotonically** (all 8 cells, Holm-corrected) while distractor-aware truncation preserves them — the protocol matters, and naive middle-dropping is "a measurement of how often middle-removal happens to spare the answer" — the leg's raw-truncated arm is the naive protocol, by design, and the field now names what it measures; (b) **length alone hurts smaller open models far more** — Llama-3.1-8B drops 24.2% at 30K even with PERFECT retrieval, while GPT-4o/Gemini hold performance: "the closed-source models generally exhibit more robustness… substantial degradation is still observed in most models" — the class-dependence is measured; (c) the **Size-Fidelity Paradox**: under lossy compression, larger compressors OVERRIDE source facts with prior beliefs ("the white strawberry → the red strawberry" — knowledge overwriting) while smaller ones reproduce verbatim — the fidelity-vs-scale curve is non-monotonic, and verbatim-preserving SUBSTRATES (the doc-chain's write-time record) are exactly the anti-overwrite mechanism. Translation: the leg's hypothesis (read-gap bites tool-shy hardest) doesn't need to be argued from first principles — the field's controlled class-comparisons already show it; the leg's contribution is measuring the MIDDLEWARE'S recovery on the hardest class, which the field's distractor-aware protocol says is the only honest measurement design.
