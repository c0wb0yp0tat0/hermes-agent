# RIG-INTENT — the night's distilled intent (plain register)

**What this is:** one read, once — the rig's intent in plain human lines, so a new reader or a new Hermes instance knows why all of this exists and what it must not become. Receipts live at the rows, never in the prose.

**Register:** plain human lines. The machinery's idiom is left in the machinery's papers.

---

## 1. THE WHY — why all of it matters

The operating successes are **one layer** of a successful pilot — not the pilot, and not the point.

The pilot has to be sustainable for 3oltan's needs. That is the test: not whether a run went green, but whether the thing keeps working, week after week, under real use, without heroics to hold it up.

And 3oltan is **not the only end game**. The pilot is what drives the innovations — it is the proving ground. What we learn there is what goes forward into everything else. The pilot exists so there is something better to build; success at the pilot is the doorway, not the house.

The as-is surface is not terrible. But it could be something wonderful. That prospect is explored as a **two-way door**: reversible, the as-is always a safe return, nothing locked in. We can walk toward the wonderful and walk back if it does not hold — the walk is the learning, and neither direction is a commitment.

## 2. THE LAYERS — five layers, each with its own role

None of these layers can be dismissed on another layer's evidence. Each one stands on its own ground.

- **The operating** — the runs, the instruments, the measurements: did it work, how well, where did it break. Its role: honest evidence about the day's work.
- **The substrate** — the kit underneath: our own surface, innovated first — never an as-is kit shipped as the answer. Its role: the material the pilot is made of. (The kvnloo lab builds theirs independently; parallel tracks, not a shared build.)
- **The pilot** — the thing run in the real place, for 3oltan's real needs, sustainable. Its role: the proving ground that drives the innovations.
- **The human** — 3oltan, and the judgment that sets the north. Its role: the need, the word, the gate. Nothing goes public without it.
- **The record** — the durable, honest account of everything: the thread, the receipts, the corrections. Its role: the memory the next shift stands on; its honesty is held, not assumed.

## 3. THE FRAME'S RULES — how to stay true to the intent

**The skew-classes — the three ways to drift, each named and each forbidden:**

1. **The pilot-as-end** — treating the pilot as the destination instead of the driver of the innovations.
2. **The innovation-as-locked-in** — treating a new shape as committed before it has earned it; forgetting the two-way door.
3. **The operating-metrics-as-the-success** — treating a green run, a converged loop, a caught class as the success itself. Metrics are one layer; they are not the pilot and not the point.

**The two-way door's mechanics:** what is reversible proceeds; what is irreversible is confirmed first. Old versions are kept; the as-is stays reachable. A next move that ships the kit as-is, or aims at the upstream lines' landings instead of the kit's innovation, is the skew-class — the north is the kit's innovation, and the landings that gate the pilot are gates, not goals. The read-restore PR's pass is the pilot's start-line: the RFC's next phase begins on the pilot after that fix passes.

**The team's ownership:**

- **The operator holds the north** — the aim, the why, the word. The frame's direction is the operator's to hold.
- **The head holds the record's honesty** — what stands as true, what is marked not-run, what was corrected. The record's trust is the head's charge.
- **The lanes do the work between** — the arms, the sweeps, the measurements, the landings. The lanes execute; they do not move the north.
