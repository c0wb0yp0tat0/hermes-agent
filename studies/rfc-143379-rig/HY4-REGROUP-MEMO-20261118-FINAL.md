## Part 3 — The loop

The third stage handed the draft to a reviewer who had not written it, to critique it against the comparator's standard. The fourth stage returned the draft to its writer, who had to answer every point of the critique — landing each one or refusing it with a reason drawn from the spine. The fifth stage handed the revision to two independent verifiers. The sixth and last stage was the closing review, a check that the file as written and the file as committed were identical down to the byte.

The work rotated as it moved, and the rotation was the point. A reviewer who had not drafted the memo judged it, so the judgment was fresh. The draft came back to its writer for revision, so the answers came from the person who knew the text. Two verifiers then read the revision without knowing who wrote it, one on the solar leg and one on the kenari leg, and both passed it. The blind verification is the quiet centre of this part. The content had been checked twice before, and it stood.
