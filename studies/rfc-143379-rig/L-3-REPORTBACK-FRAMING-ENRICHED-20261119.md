## 1 · THE TONE CONTRACT (named in rules, binding)

These rules are non-negotiable. Every sentence in the report-back is checked against them before the draft leaves the gist stage.

| Rule | What it forbids | What it requires |
|---|---|---|
| **No denouncement** | Straw-manning the dismissive post; calling anyone wrong, reckless, or foolish | The dismissal is engaged at the level it was made — a public statement — and the substantive counter is offered without character attack |
| **No "we told you so"** | Triumphalist framing; any tone that treats the NULL as proof the dismissers were wrong | The NULL is a gift, not a scorecard. It opens a question, it does not close an argument against people |
| **Credit where the engineering point is true** | Erasing the alignment on human empowerment; treating the dismissers as monolithic opponents | The alignment with Teknium1's framing on human empowerment is named and credited where the engineering point holds — this is real and it is the bridge the report-back walks across |

| **Draw for the read-gap / RFC mind** | Closing the conversation | The NULL's companion is an invitation: the read-gap class's severity on tool-shy models is the general finding the mission serves, and the RFC's read-gap minds are the natural audience for that finding |
| **Receipted, not estimated** | Vibes, speculation, or paraphrased claims | Every substantive claim traces to a named receipt; the public post's words are quoted exactly, never compressed into a strawman |
| **Reported-vs-verified vocabulary** | Treating reported numbers as final results | Every measurement is labeled: **reported** (our runs, our machines) vs **verified** (the independent rerun). Nothing public is called a result until the rerun holds. See §4 of LANE-4-BENCHREAD-20261119.md |
| **Spend reported, recovery verified** *(E2 add)* | Blurring the budget fact with the measurement fact; quoting the cap as if it were a result; letting the recovery borrow the cap's certainty; claiming cash spent when the free-surface cash is $0 | The spend row is **reported** (the pre-committed cap and the per-episode cost are our books); the recovery row is **verified-or-pending** (the rerun's). The report-back never lets the cap stand in for the finding, and states the free-surface cash honestly |

**The two-way door:** if any of these rules prove incompatible with the data that lands, the report-back is re-staged, not force-fitted. The rollback is: return to gist, revise the paragraph, re-check against the table, repeat until clean.

## 2 · THE SHAPE — what the NULL carries when the probe lands

The NULL is not a shrug. It is a structured statement with four named components.

### 2.1 The middleware's measurement (the bridged arm)
- **The channel-loss restoration:** the raw-truncated arm loses the fact (the channel effect: raw Luna truncates, the fact is gone, the seat cannot act on it). The bridged arm restores the record — the channel is rebuilt, the fact is re-observable. This is the substrate's CERTIFY class transfer: the same channel-effect shape measured on a harder seat.
- **The abstention axis:** tool-shy seats do not just answer wrong — they refuse, hedge, say "I can't." The middleware's fight: with the record restored, does the seat answer instead of abstain? The abstention rate (refusals + hedges + "I can't" per arm) is measured, not assumed.
- **The governance axis:** with the release-point governor and write-time discipline in the loop, does the seat's tool use stay inside the governed surface? The bridge is measured as compliance, not as capability. Every bridged-arm tool/record interaction carries its witness.

*(E2 add, scale receipt):* each of the three quantities is measured at the scale the economics pre-committed — **40 episodes per arm × 3 arms = 120** — and the report-back prints that n beside the quantity. The scale is a receipt, not a flourish.

*(E2 add, field vocabulary — APPENDIX-A.1/A.2):* the abstention axis speaks the field's vocabulary: the classifier's labels are ABSTAIN / HEDGE / CONFIDENT_CORRECT / CONFIDENT_WRONG, and the leg's (R+H+E)/200 is the field's EHQ1 epistemic-restraint rate — so the raw-vs-bridged delta is comparable to the panel's EHQ spread (0.31–0.81 across 21 routes). The bridged arm's governor + witness IS the middleware the field says moves the tool-call prior (A.2: "same words, different home, opposite result"); the abstention fight is the proof the prior moved.

### 2.2 The seat-neutral framing
- The point is the **middleware's agnostic quality**, not Luna's vindication. A bridge that holds on the hardest case (tool-shy, read-gap-maximized) is agnostic by demonstration — that is the finding's shape, not a Luna win.
- The dismissal's subject (Luna) is the seat, not the subject of the measurement. The measurement is of the channel, the abstention rate, and the governance compliance — three quantities that any seat can in principle produce.

*(E2 add, confidence honesty):* "agnostic by demonstration" is scoped to the seat and scale actually run. The sentence the report-back may carry is: "on this tool-shy seat, at n=40 per arm, reported" — not "agnostic" unqualified. The confidence travels with the claim.

### 2.3 The read-gap class's severity on tool-shy models (the general finding)
- The mission's upstream contribution: the read-gap (truncation destroying non-re-observable facts) bites hardest on tool-shy models. This is not a Luna-specific claim — it is a class finding, evidenced by the abstention pattern the probe instruments.
- The RFC's read-gap minds are the natural audience. The report-back names the class, names the hardest case, and points toward the conversation the RFC is already seeded for.

*(E2 add):* the class finding is the one claim in the report-back that reaches beyond the run; it is therefore labeled a **design assumption under test**, not a verified result. Its field corroboration (APPENDIX-A.3): naive truncation collapses monotonically (the raw arm is the field's naive protocol by design — it measures the channel-loss, which is the point); length alone hurts smaller open models far more than frontier (Llama-3.1-8B −24.2% at PERFECT retrieval, GPT-4o holds); larger compressors OVERWRITE facts while verbatim substrates preserve them (Size-Fidelity Paradox). The leg's contribution is the middleware's recovery on the hardest class — the n=40 run does not by itself close the class claim.

### 2.4 The counterpoint to alienation
- The substantive counter to alienation is not a denouncement of the dismissers. It is the hard data: the people who trusted the seat — who put real work into Luna, who built on it, who asked it to do things — those people's trust has an evidence base. The NULL does not say "you were right to trust Luna." It says "the engineering that produced the trust is real, and the measurement that follows is the substrate's, not the seat's."

*(E2 add, the credit-where-credit economics line):* `L-2-ECONOMICS-20261119.md` §6 hands this lane a direct receipt — "a held bridged cache-hit is a credit-where-credit line for the report-back's tone." The counterpoint to alienation therefore carries its economics seam: the engineering that produced the trust includes a held bridged cache-hit, receipted, and the report-back credits it as such.

## 3 · THE HONESTY CLAUSES

### 3.1 The honest-negative path (if the bridged arm does NOT recover the channel)
- The NULL is reported as the finding — a middleware measurement that says the bridge did not hold on this seat.
- This is still a contribution: it bounds the substrate's agnosticism claim, it falsifies the design assumption honestly, and it feeds the fleet's design loop.
- It is never spun. "The bridge did not hold on Luna" is a complete sentence. The rollback: publish the NULL, document the failure mode, return to the design loop.
- The honest-negative path does not diminish the dignity of the people who trusted the seat. Their trust is not the variable that failed — the substrate's transfer to this particular seat is.

### 3.2 The reported-vs-verified vocabulary (from LANE-4-BENCHREAD-20261119.md §4)
- Everything the report-back carries is labeled: **reported** (our runs, our machines) vs **verified** (the independent rerun). Nothing is a result until the rerun holds.
- The honest-negative path uses this vocabulary: "reported NULL on Luna at scale N; verified result pending."
- See LANE-4-BENCHREAD-20261119.md §4 for the full vocabulary contract, the door's costed routes, and the integrity argument.

### 3.3 The receipts' citations
- Every claim traces to a named receipt. The public post's words are quoted exactly, never compressed into a strawman. The abstention numbers, the governance compliance rows, and the channel-effect measurements are all sourced to the probe's ledger.
- The two-way door on citations: if a receipt is found to be misquoted or out of context, the paragraph is pulled and re-sourced before the draft advances.

### 3.4 The economics honesty clauses *(E2 add — the seam this lane enriches)*

**3.4.1 The "small-scale, never busting economics" claim, receipted.** The report-back's honesty clause is not a vibe — it is a row. It carries, verbatim from `L-2-ECONOMICS-20261119.md` §1–§2:
- the per-episode cost: **$0.406 base + 15% caching overhead = $0.467/episode**;
- the per-arm row: **40 × $0.467 = $18.68/arm; 3 arms = $56.04 paid-equivalent**;
- the total scale: **120 episodes = 56% of the pre-committed $100 cap** ($100 ≈ 214 episodes);
- the free-surface cash: **$0** — the cap is a paid-equivalent shadow class (Harvest-4 lineage), because the lane's marginal cash on the free surface is $0.
The clause reads: *"reported spend: $0.467/episode × 40/arm × 3 arms = $56.04 paid-equivalent, $0 cash on the free seat, inside the pre-committed $100 cap — a budget fact, not a result."* The two-way door: if the landed spend busts a pre-committed row (>$100 total, >$40/arm ≈ 86 episodes/arm, >200/arm), the clause is rewritten to say so — a bust is a finding, not a surprise.

**3.4.2 The scale's limits sharpen the claimed-scope language.** The NULL's recovery claim carries its n: *"the middleware recovered [X] on a tool-shy seat at n=40 per arm, reported; verification pending."* The confidence honesty rules:
- the seat is one seat; the scale is 40 episodes per arm; the arm count is the probe's;
- at n=40 a ~30% abstention rate carries ±15pp (95%), and a ≥20pp raw-vs-bridged gap is ~2 SE — measurable, not a campaign; no confidence interval is quoted beyond what the ledger computes;
- the word "recovered" is **reported**, never **verified**, until the rerun holds;
- the general class finding (§2.3) is explicitly the extrapolation the n=40 run does not by itself close.

**3.4.3 The two vocabularies do not blur.** The spend is **reported** (our books, our machines); the recovery is **verified-or-pending** (the rerun's). The report-back never writes the cap as a result, and never writes the recovery as a budget fact. Where the two meet — the cost of the verification rerun — the rerun's cost is itself a reported row, and the recovery it certifies is verified.

**3.4.4 Divergences named (never smoothed).**
- *Divergence 1 — "Receipted, not estimated" vs the pre-committed cap.* The tone contract's rule §1.6 forbids estimates; the cap is a pre-committed plan number, i.e., an estimate until the spend lands. **Resolution by evidence:** the cap is labeled *reported-plan*, a third label alongside reported/verified; it is never called a result. The divergence stands as a named labeling rule, not a contradiction.
- *Divergence 2 — §3.2 "Nothing is a result until the rerun holds" vs the economics' need to state the cap now.* The cap must be stated in the report-back's honesty close, yet it is not a result. **Resolution by evidence:** the cap is stated as *spend*, not as *finding*; the sentence structure keeps the two vocabularies in separate clauses.
- *Divergence 3 — §2.2 "agnostic by demonstration" vs the single-seat, single-scale run.* **Resolution by evidence:** the demonstration is scoped to the seat and n actually run; the unqualified word "agnostic" is not used in the draft register.
- *Divergence 4 — the free-surface cash $0 vs a report-back sentence that says "we spent $56.04."* **Resolution by evidence:** the honest phrasing is "paid-equivalent $56.04 shadow, $0 cash on the free seat" — the economics rows denominate the cap in a shadow class precisely because the marginal cash is $0.
- *Divergence 5 — §2.3's class finding reaches beyond the run vs the small-scale honesty.* **Resolution by evidence:** the class finding is labeled a design assumption under test; APPENDIX-A.3 corroborates the pattern, and the leg's n=40 does not close it.
