## 2 · One overlap

Stripped of surface, every row asks the same thing: **can the record be read again — by the agent,
after the compaction, without loss?** The read is the intersection. The compaction questions
(rows 3, 6, 10, 14, 21) ask whether the record survives its own maintenance; the provenance
questions (4, 9, 19, 20) ask whether the record's origin survives; the trust questions (11, 15,
16, 25, 26, 27, 28) ask whether the record can be tested by someone who was not there. All three
reduce to the read: a record that cannot be read again cannot be governed, provenanced, or
trusted.
