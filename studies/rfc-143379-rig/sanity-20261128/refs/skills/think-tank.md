# Think-Tank — mechanism-of-mechanisms rig orchestration

The portable schema for dispatching knowledge-building missions under the mechanism-of-mechanisms framework; the runtime/model is an adapter detail (harness-agnostic).

**Mechanism repo (source):** `/root/workspace/repos/mechanism-of-mechanisms` (git), read-only; anything undocumented = a `CREATOR-GAP`. Dispatch-contract form sheet: `references/dispatch-contract.md`.

---

## 1. The mechanism spine

The framework is a controlled learning system — NOT a fixed list of productivity tricks:

```text
classify -> choose -> probe -> evaluate -> adopt or park -> standardize -> verify
                                     ^                        |
                                     +------ meta-review ----+
```
(`CONCEPTS.md`). The **minimum runnable spine** (`workflows/minimal-run.md`): `deconstruct → sense → choose → calibrate → probe → review → record → compose-check`.

With every worker on every material task, run the minimum spine; select optional mechanisms by **value of information**, never all 40 as always-on.

## 2. The schema envelope (every dispatch)

Prime every worker (real or simulated) with `schemas/dispatch-envelope.md`:

**Operating principles:** Customer Obsession · Ownership · Bias for Action · Deliver Results · Learn and Experiment · Self-Reference.

**THE BRIEF'S ARMAMENT — the LPs and the wall clause live IN the brief (binding, earned 10-30):** every dispatch's brief carries a compact LP envelope (Customer Obsession — the finding serves the recipient, never the lab's convenience · Ownership — the seam is the lane's until receipted · Invent and Simplify · Are Right A Lot — disconfirm your own verdict before landing it · Learn and Be Curious · Bias for Action · Dive Deep · Deliver Results) AND the wall clause. THE WALL CLAUSE: a non-reproduction is a QUESTION, not a stop — the lane constructs the minimal experiment that moves the wall (advance the date, shrink the fixture, invert the condition, close the window) before it verdicts "unreproducible"; a "green here / can't reproduce" verdict without the construction is a hypothesis, and the hit-wall-without-a-construction landing means the brief never armed the LP. The home of the LPs' operative lines: `OLD-SCHOOL-AMAZON-LPS.md` in the mission-flow docs.

**THE RUSHED-BRIEF PENALTY — the brief's depth is fixed regardless of the dispatcher's mood (binding, earned 10-30):** a brief written under hurry or mood-pressure (paranoia, rush, the fear of a retread) is a DEFECT, not a shortcut — its landings are voided wholesale by the user's standard ("when they all land, consider it all rushed cheaper work and redo it ALL") and the cost is the full redo. The full ground — verbatim receipts, exact path pointers, the required receipt shapes, the LP envelope, the calibration clause — is the ONLY brief; the dispatcher's mood never enters the equation. The antidote to the rushed pass is the redo at the full standard, and the eventual cure is never having produced the thin one.

**THE EXCITEMENT LAUNCH — the parallel shotgun vs the sequenced fire (binding, earned 11-01):** the enthusiasm of a firing is the trigger to RE-CHECK the sequencing, never to skip it. Multiple lanes dispatched side-by-side collide on the wheel: the board's claims choke, and a disconfirmation lens loses the ground it must read against (the divergence is data only with the first landing present). The discipline: each landing gates the next launch, the second lens reads against the first's output, the fold after all — the user's own words: "sequenced end to end to end." The excitement's the cue the sequence needs attention, never permission to shotgun. **THE SEQUENCED SUCCESSOR SITS BLOCKED (earned 11-04):** a lane whose purpose IS the next lens (an audit-of-audit, the second codebase) is created BLOCKED on its card until the predecessor's landing — a 'ready' successor invites the dispatcher to spawn it on the first free slot and the sequence jumps (the second lens starts reading air). The block IS the sequencing's mechanical enforcement; when a jump still happens, the steer sharpens the hold-condition (the artifact path, the read-only-when-fresh, the never-improvise clause) onto the running card immediately.

**THE SETTLED SHAPES ARE THE CONTRACT — corrections ride as new passes (binding):** a shape the user has settled (a document's structure, the section list, the evidence bar, the objective lens, the register) IS the contract — set once, executed through. No re-composition mid-delivery, no "one more take" on a settled thing. **THE STEERING'S OWN ENTROPY — nurturing is the keeper-work, never the twitch (binding):** every steer costs momentum — the re-orientation, the re-context, the energy of the course-change itself — and a stream of micro-steers IS an entropy generator, not an entropy fight. **Reasoning protocol:** (1) Deconstruct the request into decision/deliverable/constraints/unknowns. (2) Evaluate the domain — **clear** → apply a standard; **complicated** → analyze with expertise; **complex** → run a bounded probe, no predictive certainty; **chaotic** → establish order first, then reclassify. (3) Synthesize evidence/alternatives/failure modes. (4) Select and justify ONE reversible next action (or why blocked). (5) Deliver.

**Required response sections:** `[Executive Summary]` · `[Analysis Brief]` (domain, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, alternatives) · `[Recommendation/Action Plan]` (one next action, its reversibility, its success criterion) · `[Next Steps/Exploration]` (follow-ups, open questions, what would change the conclusion) · `[Artifact]` (path, or BLOCKED with the exact missing input).

The ONLY exception: a mission whose object IS the thread (a translation lane, a record-audit) — the brief names the exception explicitly.
