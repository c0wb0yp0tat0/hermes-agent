# 5Y-NOTE-4 STORM DOC — "this is a two-way door" (reversibility as the field's operating discipline), 2026-11-19

*Storm doc for 5Y-NOTE-4. The note asked: why is the probe a two-way door? The storm answers with the field's own reversibility canon — the probe experiment, the sequencing rule, and the falsification-first standard — and sharpens the note: the probe must pre-commit its decision rule or it is not a probe.*

## The fan-out (2 queries, exa-served)

## The findings (mechanism per class, receipted)

**F1 — THE PROBE EXPERIMENT, DEFINED (abstractopedia.org/mechanisms/probe_experiment):** "a low-cost reversible action to learn whether an ambiguous signal is real, relevant, or accelerating" — the defining move is *acting in order to learn*, at a cost calibrated to how weak the current evidence is. Four rules: (1) name the one question; (2) design the smallest reversible action that answers it; (3) **pre-commit the decision rule** — state before running how each possible outcome updates the signal, "so the probe cannot be reinterpreted after the fact"; (4) bound it hard — fixed time, scope, spend, with a stopping rule. The discipline's spine: "if you cannot say in advance which outcome would make you drop the signal, you are not probing, you are committing." The failure modes are named: **probe creep** (the small experiment quietly grows into the commitment it was meant to test) and the **theatrical probe** (one whose result cannot change the decision). Translation: the study's probe must carry its pre-committed decision rule as part of the design — the two-arm probe's success criterion (recover majority of the 92.5-point gap at lower cost-per-success) is exactly such a rule, and the probe's bounds (episodes, arms, pennies) serve the "stay a probe" clause.

**F2 — SEQUENCING UNDER CONSTRAINT: THE REVERSIBLE MOVE GOES FIRST (delidec.com/research/sequencing-under-constraint):** every irreversible commitment spends capital AND foreclosed futures; "the right rule is to front-load the decisions you can cheaply unwind and defer the ones you cannot, because the reversible moves are where you buy information at a discount." The operator's edge: "the deliberate manufacture of cheap evidence ahead of expensive bets." The failure mode is **premature convergence** — locking hard-to-reverse decisions while cheap-to-reverse ones are still open; the tell is "a roadmap where the irreversible items cluster at the front and the experiments trail behind." Translation: the study's sequence already orders this way (the pennies-probe before the ~$100 bench door, both before any public move) — the storm confirms the ordering is the field's recommended shape, and the sequencing rule gives the mission's plan its own audit lens: sort by reversibility and information yield, never urgency.

**F3 — FALSIFICATION-FIRST: THE AGENTIC-ERA STANDARD (arXiv 2604.22080v2, "Sound Agentic Science Requires Adversarial Experiments"):** when the marginal cost of producing analyses approaches zero, the dominant failure is "plausible, endlessly revisable analyses… optimized for publishable positives." The proposal: non-experimental claims be evaluated under a falsification-first standard — the same agent that produces the analysis must also "actively search for the ways in which the claim can fail," paired with evidence that it was subjected to an adversarial trial. "The absence of falsification evidence becomes harder to justify" once agents make refutation attempts nearly free. Translation: the two-way door is not merely permitted — the field now requires the adversarial pass as evidence; the study's probe naturally satisfies this by pairing the two arms with the would-falsify reading (NOTE-1's F4 DCP zero-recovery standard is the same norm). The door's reversibility and the falsification-first standard are the same doctrine from two angles: cheap to run means cheap to refute, and both are now expected.

**F4 — PRE-REGISTRATION IS THE TWO-WAY DOOR'S PAPER FORM (arXiv 2606.11217, "Preregistration for Experiments with AI Agents"):** low marginal cost of AI-agent experiments removes the natural friction that kept specification search bounded; the field's answer is to make preregistration standard — model selection, prompt wording, decoding parameters, retry policies, parsing rules all disclosed in advance, including **pilot-testing history** ("how many prompt iterations were explored, which models were tested during development, and whether any preliminary results informed the final hypotheses"). The counter-argument — "when it costs $1 and 30 seconds to run an experiment, it is hard to see what the benefit would be" — is answered: low cost is precisely why preregistration is more necessary; "Preregistration provides the friction that cost no longer does." Translation: the probe's design doc should carry the registered-spec shape — the arms, the models, the retry policy, the decision rule, the pilot history — making the door's reversibility auditable in the field's own terms. The rig's "record-first" clause already produces the material; naming it preregistration makes it citable.

**F5 — NEGATIVE RESULTS ARE THE DOOR'S OTHER DIRECTION (arXiv 2406.03980, "Embracing Negative Results in ML"):** unpublished negative results make the community "act akin to a reinforcement learning algorithm without negative feedback" — wasted compute, repeated failures. Pre-registration (successful in life sciences) is the inefficiency cure. Translation: the probe's honest negative (the substrate does NOT recover the gap) is not a wasted door — it is the field-blessed contribution the negative lane exists to make; the two-way door's rollback is itself a publishable finding under this norm.

**F6 — THE MICRO-EXPERIMENT SEQUENCE (abstractopedia.org/mechanisms/micro_experiment_sequence):** chains small cheap tests into a branching series where each result reroutes the next experiment — "the sequence as a whole learns something no single experiment could" — with memory across steps and branches chosen from the accumulated trace. Failure mode: HARKing across the sequence (deciding what you were testing only after seeing which branch looked good) — the guard is pre-committing each step's hypothesis and success bar. Translation: the 5Y-notes' storm-seeds themselves form a micro-experiment sequence — each note's storm is a probe whose result reframes the next; the notes' sequential documentation IS the running trace. The HARKing guard names the discipline the series must hold: each note's decision rule stated before its storm, which these storm-docs (with their pre-stated questions and receipted findings) already do.

## The refinement to the 5Y (what this changes)
- The two-way door's strength is not just cheapness — it is the pre-committed decision rule (F1): the probe doc must state in advance which outcome drops the signal, or the probe is a commitment wearing a probe's clothes.
- The door's ordering is field-canon: reversible first, irreversible last, each irreversible move contingent on a probe that already reported (F2) — the mission's sequence (probe → bench door → public) is the canonical arrangement.
- The adversarial pass (F3) and preregistration (F4) upgrade the probe's contract: the two arms' falsification reading is now a field-required evidence class, and the design doc's spec disclosure makes the door auditable. The negative result (F5) is a contribution, not a loss.
- The notes series itself is the micro-experiment sequence (F6): branch from what each storm revealed, pre-commit each next step's bar.
