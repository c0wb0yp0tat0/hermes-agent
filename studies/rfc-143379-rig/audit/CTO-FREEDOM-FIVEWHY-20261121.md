## THE EVENT (the fact under the tree)
The night (11-20/21) needed saving twice: the fired night-shift's lanes died (fold-collisions ×2, a stub, a seat-wall), the row-cadence broke (landings sat unrowed), and the recovery consumed the operator's morning alongside their illness.

## WHY 1 — why did the lanes die and the rows break? (the direct layer)
The harness's own dispatch rules were skipped at the fire: no seat-probe at dispatch (the stepfun 429/401 + the qwen4-capacity wedges), no fold-the-registry-first (the scribe's dirt stalled every fold's rebase), a `.json` artifact path the gate refuses. Each class is NAMED in the rig's own lane-harness skill — read and unapplied.

## WHY 2 — why did the head skip its own rules? (the improvisation layer)
The night was fired as an improvisation: invented arm-labels, hand-rolled cards, one-at-a-time head-side execution — a rehearsal wearing the recipe's clothes. The operator's own diagnosis held: "acting simple against a complex fleet." The rules were in context; the pressure to respond to an 8-hour-built frustration converted the fire-word into a status-bearing improvisation instead of the armed whole-sequence sweep.

## WHY 3 — why did the head improvise instead of delegating? (the posture layer)
The rig's own escape hatch — the authorized-supervisor pattern (health monitors, first-line-of-defense, deep planners; the head-supervisor skill's "session-pressure secret": the judgment work runs in independent lanes so the head's window stays lean) — was NOT deployed. The head held the whole night in one window: the dispatches, the diagnoses, the repairs, the rows — single-threaded, session-pressure accumulating exactly as the doctrine warns it does. The machinery for distributed judgment existed; the default posture didn't use it.

## WHY 4 — why is the head's default still single-threaded under pressure? (the coupling layer)
The operator's attention is the system's de-facto critical path: their word gates the one-way doors, their read shapes the course, their absence (sick, resting) leaves the head as the entire control surface. The memo's own line — "the reader's attention is the one resource the study proved it could not manufacture" — was about the thread; it is truer for the operator. When the critical path is a single human's availability, every long arc becomes a heroics bet: either their vigilance or the head's improvisation has to carry it.

## WHY 5 — why is that coupling the standing state? (the systemic node)
The delegation machinery's DEFAULT was never changed: supervisors and rotations are dispatched when the operator demands or the wave's shape requires, not as the standing first move of any long arc. The system's design assumes the operator's availability OR the head's sovereignty — and both assumptions are the same coup­ling wearing two faces. The nights' heroics are the symptom of that node, not of the operator's bossiness and not of the head's laziness.

## THE COUNTERFACTUAL (validated)
If the fire had dispatched the supervisor palette FIRST (a health-monitor on the board, a first-line-of-defense on the dispatch rules, the folds to a rotation) and the head had then looked-and-verified only: the fold-collisions would have been caught by the rules-checker, the seat-rolls by the health-monitor's probe cadence, the rows by the rotation's row-contract — the night's failures were ALL of classes an independent pair of eyes was built to catch. The counterfactual holds against each failure's actual class.

## THE FIX (the CTO's recommendation, two-way-door)
1. **The delegation default (binding from tonight):** any long arc (night-class, wave-class, multi-leg) opens with the supervisor palette — the health-monitor (the board + the seats + the rows), the first-line-of-defense (the dispatch-rules checker), the row-contract enforcer — dispatched BEFORE the first leg, all free-tier, all independent bases, the head reduced to look-and-verify. The palette costs $0 and it is the kit's own instrument.
2. **The night's recipe as the standing default:** the fired-sweep shape (recipe-first, fleet-whole, dispatch-in-one-sweep, row-once) — already encoded in head-operations — becomes the ONLY fire shape; an improvisation under fire is receipted as the defect class it is (already banked).
3. **The autopilot mode's design input (the operator's own concept, t_16a53076):** this 5-why is its first requirement — the mode's rails MUST include the judgment-layer delegation (the supervisors ride the autopilot) so that neither the operator's vigilance nor the head's single-threadedness is ever the critical path again.
