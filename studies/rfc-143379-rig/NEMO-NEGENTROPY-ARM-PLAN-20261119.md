## THE FAILURE MODES + AUTO-PATHS
1. **Lane Aborts:**
   - **Failure Mode:** A lane aborts due to unstandardized rules.
   - **Auto-Path:** The head re-arms the lane with the standardized rules and continues.

2. **Seat 429s:**
   - **Failure Mode:** A seat returns a 429 error due to stale supply or machine-layer issues.
   - **Auto-Path:** The dispatch re-surveys the supply and re-checks the machine layer before retrying.

3. **Unreachable Scribe Cell:**
   - **Failure Mode:** A scribe cell becomes unreachable due to machine-layer failures.
   - **Auto-Path:** The scribe tunnel is re-established, and the cell is re-verified.

4. **Branch Head Moves:**
   - **Failure Mode:** The branch head moves without the watchers updating the loop-state token.
   - **Auto-Path:** The watchers re-align the loop-state token with the new branch head.

5. **Watcher and Landing Disagree:**
   - **Failure Mode:** A watcher and landing disagree on the loop-state token or fold verification.
   - **Auto-Path:** The head mediates the disagreement and enforces the standardized rules.
