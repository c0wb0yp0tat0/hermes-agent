The efficient invent-and-simplify ACES-loop runs until no more measurable improvement is achieved. It consists of the following stages:

1. **Invent-Pass**: Examine the broken-arrow approaches to identify what to simplify, extend, or combine.
2. **Measure**: Measure each candidate's improvement using the batteries' arms, comparing before and after receipts.
3. **Gate**: Stop the loop when no measurable improvement is detected, based on the mechanical receipts (lane-landings' bytes, fresh-tests' rows, batteries' outputs).
4. **Receipts**: Record every loop's verdict and the total recorded receipts.

## Battery-Doctrine

The plan leans heavily on the self-hosted battery (un-metered spine) and uses the cloud battery only when additional matrix is needed.
