# THE WALK-PAIR SHAPING — ENRICHED

## **THE MISSION**
**Objective:** Shape the walk-pair into a dispatch-ready plan with two arcs (ARC-FORWARD and ARC-BACKWARD) intersecting at an **AoA (Area of Agreement)**. The plan includes:

1. **ARC-FORWARD** specifics: First three legs (seats, artifacts, battery-arms).
2. **ARC-BACKWARD** specifics: First three legs (historical nights audited, seats, keyless-carried lines).
3. **THE MEET**: Choreography of the crossing, AoA-checks, and post-crossing continuity.
4. **THE ASSETS**: Batteries, keyless rotation, and recovery-parser integration.
5. **THE RECEIPTS**: Proof of leg claims (falsifier-forms, matrix arms).

---

## **THE GEOMETRY**
- **ARC-FORWARD**: A forward-moving arc with three legs, each riding a distinct battery-arm.
- **ARC-BACKWARD**: A backward-moving arc auditing historical nights, carrying keyless lines.
- **THE MEET**: A central crossing where both arcs converge, validate AoA-checks, and continue.
