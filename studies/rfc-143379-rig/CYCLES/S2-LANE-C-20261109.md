## 5-Why Depth-Clause: Why G5–G7 Populate is the S2 FAN's Canonical Step

1. **Why does S2 FAN need G5–G7 populated?** Because S3 FOLD fires only when per-leg receipts are present, and G5–G7 are the study-ledger's forward-fields that generate those receipts.
2. **Why are G5–G7 the study-ledger's forward-fields?** Because G5 carries the live-finding-arcs rosters (the probe-ring landings), G6 carries the compounding-arcs seeds (the successor arcs), and G7 carries the fleet-baseline rows (each seat's last-landed + next-arm) — together they are the complete forward state from which S3 splits and S4 checks derive.
3. **Why must the probe-rings land ≥3 candidates in HOUR1-RING-A?** Because S1 ARM's hour-1 six-item shape produces the canonical probes; fewer than three would break the dependency-collapse guard (parallel-armed-gates/total-gates ≥0.5 by fold-2).
4. **Why must fan-out be ≥2 per fold in G6?** Because the amplification engine enforces compounding-rate ≥2 at every fold; <2 flags the fold SOFT with budget cost 1, never dismissed.
5. **Why must the fleet-baseline rows track last-landed + next-arm?** Because swap-rotation in S3 re-arms seats per evidence; without last-landed and next-arm, recursive-breadth (splits_n+1 = 2×splits_n) and fleet-scaling (concurrency ≥1.5× per phase) cannot be measured or enforced.

**Webstorm ride-the-landing:** the landing is S3 FOLD at t=90. G5–G7 populate is the only S2 step that produces all three receipt classes (per-leg, fold, grader) the S3 fast-cadence needs. The webstorm's cross-look rides the landing against the adjacent fields: `S2-LANE-B`'s verification-gate yellow (the witness line is fold-bound — G6's ARC-G6-12 owns its landing), `S2-LANE-D`'s 7-row SOFT pool (G6's seeds are the input the fold's splits pull), and `RING-F4`'s outside-scope boundaries (the F2 cert + the canary run-receipt are owed — G6's ARC-G6-05/06 and ARC-G6-08 own their closure).
