## 5-why (depth-clause)

1. Why one seed per SOFT item? Because the S3 split's contract (orchestration §3) arms one seed per SOFT-pool row; the queue row is the seed's handle.
2. Why seed-status per row? Because S3 must know whether evidence permits the fan now (ready-for-fan) or whether a definition must land first (needs-definition) — the split arms on evidence, not on hope.
3. Why owner-candidate per row? Because each successor-arc needs a named role (METER-BUS / GATE-KEEPER / FANOUT-HANDLER / lane) to carry it; an unowned seed is a dropped seed.
4. Why evidence-pointer per row? Because the grader clause counts only graded claims with a receipt on disk; an unpointed seed is ungraded and does not count toward standing.
5. Why never drop a row? Because SOFT items are budget-cost-1 inputs to the next fold's splits, not failures; dropping one silently starves the amplification engine of a compounding input.
