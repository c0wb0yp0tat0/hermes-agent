## 4. Depth-clause — 5-why + webstorm

**Why 1 — Why swap at all instead of re-running same seats on same tasks?**
Because orchestration §3's amplification engine requires fan-out-per-finding ≥2 and recursive-breadth doubling per phase; re-running identical tasks yields fan-out 1, which flags the fold SOFT (budget cost 1) and stalls compounding. The swap rotates seats onto the evidence's own gaps so each finding spawns ≥2 successor-arcs.

**Why 2 — Why map thinnest→densest and strongest→audit-side (not random or round-robin)?**
Thinnest landing (D, SOFT) signals the seat already carries unresolved load; giving it the densest next-task converts the SOFT into split-queue work (recursive-breadth) rather than letting it rot. Strongest landing (A, PASS) signals capacity to verify others; moving it to audit-side supplies the grader (external byte-direct audit / independent witness / certifying re-run) that ungraded meters lack. Round-robin would strand the audit function and leave SOFT seeds unowned.

**Why 3 — Why do the three evidence-gaps own the three mid seats (B, C, E) rather than the strong/weak seats?**
The gaps are confirmation/definition/scaling work that requires the seat that produced the evidence (B produced gate-scan → owns needs-definition seed; C produced byte-keyed G5–G7 → owns fingerprint-integrity; E produced fleet-baseline → owns phase-bound scaling). Moving a different seat would break the byte-keyed provenance chain and the independent-witness line. The strong seat (A) is busy auditing; the weak seat (D) is busy executing the densest load.

**Why 4 — Why is fingerprint-integrity "unconfirmed" rather than "failed"?**
The byte-keyed G5–G7 leg (`c649708e51`) populated 3/3 fields but did not carry an independent-witness confirmation of fingerprint integrity within the fold window. Per orchestration §3 grader clause, an ungraded meter does not count; hence it is unconfirmed, not failed, and is routed to seat C for confirmation rather than to a SOFT-flagged re-run. This keeps the fold's budget cost at the SOFT already paid by D.

**Why 5 — Why does this swap keep the loop from terminating on zero-delta?**
C24d zero-delta check (S5) fires only if measured delta=0 across a fold. This swap emits successor-arcs (B→seed resolution, C→fingerprint confirmation, E→scaling re-measure, D→split doubling) on top of A's audit-side, so fan-out-per-finding ≥2 is structurally supplied; zero-delta is therefore not the expected path, and the loop continues S3→S4 rather than terminating. If any leg lands delta=0, that single receipt is reported honestly (no inflation) and S5→S6 terminal is accepted, not treated as failure.
