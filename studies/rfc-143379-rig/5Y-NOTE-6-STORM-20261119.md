## The findings (mechanism per class, receipted)

**F1 — MULTIPLE DISCOVERY IS THE NORM, NOT THE EXCEPTION (Merton; kk.org "Simultaneous Invention"; SN Social Sciences 2026 "A systemic theory of scientific discovery"):** Robert K. Merton's "multiples" — similar discoveries made by scientists working independently — dominate the history of science; simultaneous invention is the norm (Kelvin, Darwin, Newton, the calculus). The systemic theory names the mechanism: **a field reaches readiness** (conceptual, technical, empirical, social, institutional) and "makes some ideas thinkable, expressible, and stable" — then multiple groups converge because they work inside the same system of problems and resources. Translation: the convergence is NOT coincidence and NOT copying — it is the field-readiness signal. The rig's shapes (doc-chain, write-time assert, governed compaction, verdict loops) became independently discoverable because the substrate question reached readiness — which is the field's own confirmation that the problem is real and load-bearing, not a rig quirk.

**F2 — THE FIELD NOW NAMES THE HARNESS THE BOTTLENECK (arXiv 2605.26112, "From Model Scaling to System Scaling"):** "agent performance emerges from the interaction among the foundation model, memory substrate, context constructor, skill-routing layer, orchestration loop, and verification-and-governance layer — together, these form the agent harness" — and harness scaling is "the next major bottleneck," with three core problems: **context governance, trustworthy memory, dynamic skill routing.** The observation on three production harnesses (Claude Code, OpenClaw, CheetahClaws): "each addresses context governance, memory management, and skill routing… this convergence suggests that they are intrinsic design problems for agentic AI systems, rather than incidental features." Translation: the rig's RFC (read-restore + governed compaction) sits exactly on the field's named bottleneck pair — context governance and trustworthy memory — and the field's "converged because intrinsic" is the receipt for NOTE-6's root.

NOTE-6's "independent discovery" claim is corroborated by the theory (F1) and by the field's own convergent designs (F2, F3).
