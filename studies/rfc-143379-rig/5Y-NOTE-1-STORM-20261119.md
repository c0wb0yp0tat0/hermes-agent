## The findings (mechanism per class, receipted)

**F1 — THE CERTIFICATION LADDER EXISTS, AND IT NAMES OUR MISSING RUNG (arXiv 2610.30549, "AI-Native Academia Should Build a Community Verification Layer"):** the field's tiered ladder: 0 relevance note → 1 comprehension → 2 claim check → 3 technical audit → 4 artifact execution → 5 reproduction → 6 **independent replication** → 7 downstream validation. The rig's batteries + stress floors are tiers 4–5 (artifact execution, reproduction on own machines); the RFC's jfugalde/Finn763 confirmations are tier 3–5 (independent but partial); **tier 6 — independent replication — is the stamp M1 names, and the field's own ladder ranks it above everything the study currently holds.** Continual-ARC's verified (50 private rules, undisclosed seed) IS tier 6. The mission's "certify" verb now has an exact ladder-position target.

**F2 — CLAIM CERTIFICATION IS AN ARCHITECTURE, NOT A HOPE (EviBound, arXiv 2511.05524):** dual governance gates: pre-execution approval (acceptance-criteria schema validated before code runs) + post-execution verification (artifacts via queryable run_id, FINISHED status). **Baseline A (prompt-only): 100% hallucination (8/8 claimed, 0/8 verified). Baseline B (verification-only): 25%. EviBound (dual gates): 0% — at ~8.3% execution overhead.** Translation: the rig's write-time scribe assert + record-first clause is EviBound's post-gate already; the pre-gate (acceptance criteria ratified BEFORE the run — the RFC's unratified criteria, Kettering item 5) is the missing half. "Claims propagate only when backed by a queryable run_id" is the doc-chain's provenance line, field-encoded.

**F3 — AGREEMENT IS NOT EVIDENCE (VeriHarness, google-research, 2026):** the verifier's power comes not from a stronger judge but from structure: N independent rollouts, a disagreement-resolver (run the separating check against the environment), and a consensus-challenger ("Agreement is not evidence: rollouts share a model and therefore blind spots" — seek ways a shared value/reading/omission could be wrong). Evidence-backed revision: +6.2 to +6.4 points over single-rollout baselines. Translation: the rig's stranger-re-read + 3-4 adversarial pushbacks + AoA-on-different-base IS VeriHarness's resolver+challenger; "agreement is not evidence" is the exact principle behind the CTO-lens + no-lens closing ritual. The mission's certification argument can cite this as the field's formalization of the review-loop clause.

**F4 — THE DISCOVERY CLAIM NEEDS RECOVERY-CONTROL (arXiv 2609.09219, Discovery Certification Protocol):** a claim becomes certified when matched agents given the same starting info + observed Web bytes do NOT recover the result: zero recoveries in 96 attempts, bounded at 0.0468. "Scores alone do not prove discovery." Confirmatory runs cost $54–$61 per audit — the pennies-scale again. Translation: the field's falsifier for a claimed finding is the matched-challenger test with fresh measurements withheld — the inverse of our probe's two arms; the DCP is the drop-in certification protocol for any rig claim that wants tier 6 without a third-party bench.

**F5 — VERIFIER-GENERATION IS ITSELF BENCHMARKED, AND FALSE REJECTION IS THE HIDDEN FAILURE (VerifierBench, arXiv 2610.28679):** generated verifiers reject invalid solutions more reliably than they accept valid ones — most false rejections come from verifiers that accept NO tested solution. V4V's six checks (replay, repeatability, provenance, input-label consistency, trivial baselines, mutation testing) are the audit-of-the-verifier. Translation: the probe's acceptance battery must test "the verifier accepts a correct solution" AND "accepts other permitted solutions" — the RFC's re-read idempotence + the concurrency survivors are this shape; the hidden failure class (overly restrictive verification) is the caution for the probe's success criterion: a probe that certifies by rejecting everything is a false-negative trap.

**F6 — STAKE-LEVEL AUDITABILITY IS THE BOTTLENECK (arXiv 2602.13855, From Fluent to Verifiable):** as research generation becomes cheap, auditability becomes the bottleneck — "the real cost is not reading, but tracing." The AAR standard: provenance coverage, provenance soundness, contradiction transparency, audit effort. Semantic provenance graphs with continuous validation during synthesis. Translation: the study's docs (every claim traced to a receipt, "not confirmed" where unreadable) are claim-level auditability already practiced; the mission's documentation series IS the provenance graph the field proposes — the RFC's provenance-origin classification has a field-standard (PROV) to cite.

## The refinement to the 5Y (what this changes)
- The "might work" claim strengthens: the certification ladder is built, the price of tier 6 is ~$100 (Continual-ARC) or $54–$61 (DCP self-audit), and the rig already runs most of the architecture (write-assert, review-loop, provenance) — the mission's missing piece is now precisely named: **the independent-replication tier + the ratified pre-gate**, and the field offers two doors to it (bench verify, DCP self-audit) at pennies-scale.
