## The findings (mechanism per class, receipted)

**F1 — THE SEALED-EXAM SHAPE IS THE FIELD'S RECOMMENDED PRACTICE (arXiv 2510.07575 "Benchmarking is Broken"; arXiv 2603.23292 "LLM Olympiad"):** the cure for manufactured scores is the sealed protocol: problems sealed until evaluation, **submissions frozen in advance, one standardized harness, artifacts released after for audit.** "The key shift is that the benchmark is not only 'the dataset,' but the procedure." Translation: Continual-ARC's verify contract (code frozen at a commit, 50 private rules at an undisclosed seed, their harness reruns it) IS the field-recommended LLM-Olympiad protocol exactly. The door is not an exception to integrity practice — it is the field's recommended exemplar. A verified run on it carries the field's strongest epistemic weight (only behind a fully sealed independent event).

**F2 — THE CONTAMINATION TAXONOMY DEFINES OUR SUBMISSION'S INTEGRITY CHECKLIST (arXiv 2608.29463):** five types, each survives the prior type's remedy: **Type 1 direct** (the item — closed by holding it out), **Type 2 derivative** (the source document — provenance control), **Type 3 temporal** (the resolved outcome — cutoff control), **Type 4 distributional** (the pattern — regeneration control), **Type 5 acquired** (the evaluation run itself — sanitization, isolation, transcript review; 5a environmental, 5b retrieval, 5c adversarial). **"Holding out a private test set closes the first of these alone."** Type 5 "must be certified per run, not per benchmark" — "a certification of evaluation integrity cannot rest on the compliance of the system under test." Translation: the door's contract lines map one-to-one onto the taxonomy — the 50-rule private set closes Type 1; the rule "no ARC/Re-ARC data inside the learner, its prompts, or its starting memory" closes Types 2–3; the deterministic public stream + fresh private seed closes Type 4; **the input-logging-OFF-or-void clause is Type 5b's control (retrieval during the run)** — and the destroy-on-leak note (their logs would hold our held-out tasks) is the taxonomy's "the held-out set is compromised the moment it becomes reachable online." Our submission's integrity story can be written as a per-type closing argument, field-term by field-term.

**F3 — THE REPORTED-vs-VERIFIED DISTINCTION IS THE FIELD'S HONESTY-INSTRUMENT (Unify's own contract; arXiv 2609.09219 DCP):** a pull-request result is "reported, a claim, and it becomes a result only once we have rerun its code on the held-out set." The field's parallel: DCP — "Scores alone do not prove discovery"; certification requires matched-challenger recovery tests with fresh measurements withheld. Translation: the door's two-step surface (reported → verified) forces exactly the study's record-first discipline — nothing the rig posts is a result until the independent rerun says so. The study's own habit (label reported vs verified) is the field's norm on this surface, and the door's vocabulary gives it the exact words.

**F4 — THE COST OF THE STAMP IS THE CHEAPEST CERTIFICATION ON THE BOARD (benchmark-integrity economics):** retro-holdout construction for one benchmark costs substantial human expert time (arXiv 2410.09247, Benchmark Inflation); LeakScale's causal exposure measurement ran 589,824 generations (arXiv 2609.27176); CapBencher offers the Bayes-accuracy alarm trick (proceedings.mlr.press/v306) as a cheaper substitute. Against those, **Continual-ARC's ~$100 Luna-class public-stream + verify is the field's cheapest certified stamp** — one order cheaper than DCP's $54–$61 self-audits, two orders cheaper than building a retro-holdout. The door is not merely open; it is the economic optimum the integrity canon offers.

**F5 — WHAT THE INTEGRITY CANON SAYS THE DOOR REWARDS (the submission that passes):** per arXiv 2603.23292 — "strong performance cannot be manufactured through benchmark-specific optimization or test-set exposure, and every score comes with a public trail of evidence—tasks, code, and logs—that the community can verify." Per arXiv 2605.19999 — closing benchmarks risks "stifling innovation by increasing barriers to independent verification," so the survivors are the open-code systems. Translation: the reward is for the system whose claim is carried by its own reproducible code — which is the study's whole posture (record-first, receipts on disk, the fork's public repo). The door rewards exactly what the rig already is.

**F6 — THE DIAL + THE EFFORT ARE LEGITIMATE VARIABLES (the taxonomy's excluded-yet-allowed line):** the door's contract explicitly allows "memory, programs, tools, a cache, training the weights; compute logged and shown beside the score, never in it." Translation: the harness-settings-as-class-defaults clause and the effort knob are legal surface for the submission — the study's probe can ride the dial (start low, escalate) inside the door without touching the integrity line. What is illegal is only ARC-data-inward — a Type 1–4 blocker, exactly as the taxonomy draws it.
