## 1. THE DESIGN-SPACE SPEC

**Theorem-bearing space:** the design space is searched programmatically (evolution over designs, not hand-picking), and proven composition modules are transplanted across programs. Concretely: the rig's inventions are cataloged as programs; each program carries a mechanism set, a probe set, and a used-feature set. A variant is generated by swapping one mechanism (the composition-module transplant shape); the search loop is variant → probe → measured verdict → adopt/park. The F_used/held-out axis measures whether a probe exercises features already touched by prior probes (used) or a brand-new mechanism never probed (held-out, the discovery instrument).

**Inventions cataloged as programs** (seed spec, not built):

- **Write-gate** — committed write gate: a predicate/commit step gates on a measured signal (e.g., a learned signal) before the write commits; replay on rejection. Mechanisms: predicate, signal source, replay buffer, verdict logger. Probes: gate-hit rate, rejection rate, verdict-latch. Landmark verdict: write-gate rejection verdict.
- **Batteries** (thrifty/memory-economy) — distributed throwaway / carry-forward storage: a battery is held when the measured signal says "do not discard"; a new probe writes the battery's contents and re-detects. Mechanisms: throwaway policy, carry-forward condition, probe writer. Probes: battery-hit rate, discard verdict, re-detect rate. Landmark verdict: battery carry-forward verdict.
- **SPA-DREAM** (crystallized dream-style shaping) — shaping from a learned/frozen distribution: a dream-like proposal is used to bias the next variant's mechanism. Mechanisms: frozen distribution, shaping bias, proposal backoff. Probes: shaping-accept rate, proposal-backoff rate, verdict drift. Landmark verdict: dream-shaping verdict.
- **Sustainability recipe** — a recipe that keeps the rig's economy sustainable; mechanisms: cost per probe, budget, recycling of proven modules. Probes: cost-per-probe, budget-stop, recipe-viable. Landmark verdict: recipe-ok verdict.

**Evolution operator:** swap one mechanism from a parent design with a proven mechanism from the sibling design (the composition-module transplant shape). Variants carry the probe-set of the host design plus the newly swapped mechanism. The probe-set is re-run in the host.

**Search loop (the mechanism spine):** generation → variant synthesis (swap) → probe evaluation (named verdicts) → adopt if verdict green and measured; park if red or unverified (the adopt-without-measure class is a FLAG). Repeat until yield or budget stop.

## 2. THE PROBE SHAPE (THE FALSIFIER)

Seeded evolution experiment:

(a) Seed the current inventions (two cataloged designs above), each with its mechanisms and a used-feature set (the features that prior probes of that invention already exercised).

(b) One generation:
- **variant-A:** swaps mechanism X of design A with mechanism Y from design B (transplant shape).
- **variant-B:** transplants module Y from the sibling design into design A (the second transplant shape).

(c) Measured criteria:
- The variants' probes run with named verdicts.
- The **F_used probes** exercise the used features with the known verdicts reproduced (the falsifier battery's F_used category).
- The **held-out probe** (a mechanism never probed before) produces a NEW verdict (the discovery signal).

(d) FLAG condition: if a variant lands without its probe's verdict (the adopt-without-measure class), or the held-out probe reproduces the used verdict exactly (the taxonomy failed to discriminate), the probe is FLAGged. The battery home is `fixes/falsifier-battery-20261104` (the F_used probe category).

## 3. THE TRANSPLANT TERM

A composition-module transplant crosses designs only with its receipts: the module's probe verdicts ride the module. On landing in the new design, the transplant's landing re-probes the module (the module's promise is re-verified in the host). A transplant's failure is the module's finding — never the host's blame. The host's probes only judge the host's behavior.

## 4. THE METERS

- **evolution-yield:** variants probed / variants generated.
- **transplant-success:** transplanted modules' re-probes green / transplanted.
- **discovery-rate:** held-out probes with new verdicts / held-out probes run.
- **used-reproduction:** used probes reproducing known verdicts / used probes run.
- **run record:** `[ABSENT]` unless the fold's execution lands.
