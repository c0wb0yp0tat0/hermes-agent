## 1. THE SERVING SPEC

### 1.1 Seat model — the rig's serving surface

The serving surface is two-layer: the **Harbor `:44831` ticker seat** (the rig's local short-task seat, this probe's battery home) and the **fleet's seat roster** (the candidate responders the rotation-watch sweeps). Every seat carries a **liveness receipt**: `receipt_s = {seat_id, t_probe, outcome_class, ttl}` where `outcome_class ∈ {200-fresh, 429-throttled, 404-gone, timeout}`. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (battery home + roster named in the brief; receipt shape is this lane's adaptation)

- **Hot states** = seats holding a fresh (non-decayed) receipt AND a favorable admission score `rho_s` (§1.3). Hot states are served first; their conversation/KV state is the thing the agent is about to touch — pre-warmed, first in the serving order.
- **Cold states** = everything else: decayed receipts, 429-throttled, 404-gone, never-probed. Cold states rest — no pre-warm, no admission, no assumption of life. They wait for a fresh probe to say otherwise.

This is the field mechanism (agent-aware KV manager) moved onto the roster: the serving layer keeps its hot set aligned with the agent's actual access pattern, not with a remembered list. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

### 1.2 PD-split adaptation — the split follows the seat's demand pattern

Serving work splits into **predict** (state build / prefill-analog: batching the seat's incoming context into a servable state) and **decode** (token-step analog: draining the response). The split is *not fixed*. Per cadence it adapts to the measured demand pattern of the seat:

- **Measure:** arrival inter-arrival mean `μ`, arrival burstiness (variance/mean), and observed P95 TTFT over the cadence's probes and tasks at the battery seat. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (the field's class measures latency + P95 TTFT)
- **Adapt:** split ratio `θ = predict_share / decode_share`, updated once per cadence:
  `θ_{t+1} = clip( θ_t + k · (P95_target − P95_meas) / P95_target , θ_min , θ_max )`
  When arrivals cluster at sweep boundaries (bursty, short-horizon ticker traffic) the controller raises `θ` — more budget to predict-batching the burst into hot states, less to decode-draining short outputs. When traffic flattens, `θ` relaxes back. `k` is a small gain; `θ_min/θ_max` bound the search. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`
- **Why:** this is the field's headline result — adaptive splitting bought 4.1× latency and 14× P95 TTFT against the non-adaptive baseline. The rig's proxy for "demand pattern" is the ticker seat's publish cadence; the proxy for the speedups is the meter set in §4 (admission precision + re-pin latency), because live TTFT is not readable on this lane (no reads). `[INLINE-CRATE]` for the field numbers; `[ABSENT]` for live TTFT (contract).

What adapts each cadence: `θ`, the admission order, the decay clock. What never adapts at runtime: roster membership (compile-time, §1.4a), receipt semantics (stage-decoupled, §1.4b), the three-state grid (§1.4d). `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

### 1.3 Agent-aware admission — hot served first, arbitrated by rho

Admission order is rebuilt every cadence by sorting seats on the MemCredit-style arbitration score, applied here at the bank = the concurrency budget `b` (how many seats may be simultaneously hot):

`rho_s = ( V_s − λ · H_s ) / b`

- `V_s` — expected value of admitting seat `s`: the ticket/task value it would serve if admitted now (fresh probe passed ⇒ high `V`; decayed-only ⇒ `V` collapses toward zero).
- `H_s` — entropy/uncertainty of the seat's liveness: fresh same-cadence probe ⇒ low `H`; remembered-only (decayed) ⇒ high `H`; never probed ⇒ maximal `H`.
- `λ` — risk-aversion constant (how hard uncertainty taxes the score).
- `b` — the bank: concurrency slots available this cadence. Dividing by `b` makes the score budget-aware; when `b` tightens, only the highest-value, most-certain seats clear the bar.

**Order of service:** descending `rho_s`. Hot seats (fresh receipts, low `H`) top the order and are served first; cold seats fall below the line and rest. A seat cannot buy its way up the order on memory alone — the `H` term is exactly the tax on stale knowledge. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (MemCredit's rho=(V−λH)/b is the named bearing; `V/H/λ/b` roles are this lane's mapping onto seats); `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

This is the agent-aware half of the target mechanism: the serving layer admits in the order the agent will actually need, and it pays an explicit uncertainty tax for anything it only *remembers*.

### 1.4 The four misc, mapped onto the serving surfaces

**(a) Compile-time gating.** Roster membership is decided at fire-time, before the cadence runs: the seat list is *compiled*, and compilation fails loudly if a declared seat lacks its probe artifacts. Runtime never admits a seat on vibes ("it was live yesterday"); runtime only executes the compiled admission order against fresh evidence. The current roster's compile-time checks are the template: presence is a compile-time property, never a runtime feeling. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (misc class named in the brief); `[DESIGN-NOTE]` (extended to roster membership)

**(b) Stage-decoupled evaluation.** The probe stage and the landing stage are separate stages; each emits its own receipt. **Probe receipt** = who answered, outcome class, timestamp. **Landing receipt** = what disposition landed (auto/hold/chair), on which seat, against which probe id. Neither stage's validity borrows from the other: a failed probe cannot be papered over by a warm landing, and a clean landing cannot retro-clean a failed probe. CoMem's fidelity-ladder L2 selection is the rationale — the probe is the cheap L1 filter, the landing is the committed L2 record. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

**(c) Validity decay.** A liveness receipt decays. Half-life `h = C` (one cadence; §3.2 for the AIMD schedule). After decay the receipt is inert: it cannot gate admission, cannot gate a re-pin, cannot vote in `rho` beyond its maximal `H` penalty. A decayed receipt is evidence of nothing — validity decays; trust does not persist. At fire-time the seat is re-probed, never trusted. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (misc class); `[DESIGN-NOTE]` (h=C placement)

**(d) Three-state delegation — the fail-safe grid.** Every disposition lands in exactly one of three states, each with a receipt:

| probe outcome | disposition | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 200-fresh, rho clears the bar | **AUTO-CONTINUE** | seat healthy; the cadence continues through it |
| 429-throttled | **HOLD-SAFE** | honor retry-after; pause that flow until a re-probe clears it |
| 404-gone / re-pin fails within cadence | **NEEDS-THE-CHAIR** | escalate; the chair decides re-pin, retire, or replace |
| timeout | **HOLD-SAFE**, then NEEDS-THE-CHAIR if the next sweep repeats | transient-first, escalate-on-repeat |

The grid is fixed — it does not adapt, negotiate, or vibe. Every cell lands with `{state, seat_id, probe_id, ts}`. This is the delegation model's three states (auto / hold / defer-to-chair) made concrete as a probe-outcome function. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (three-state delegation named in the brief); `[DESIGN-NOTE]` (grid contents)

### 1.5 The serving spec in one sentence

Per cadence: compile the roster, probe every seat, classify fresh/throttled/gone/timed-out, sort survivors by `rho=(V−λH)/b`, serve hot first with the adapted split `θ`, decay every receipt by its half-life, and land every disposition on the three-state grid with a receipt — and never let a remembered-live seat skip the fresh probe. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

## 2. THE PROBE SHAPE (the falsifier)

### 2.1 The seeded serving experiment

Runs at fire-time over exactly one cadence `C` — the rotation-watch's sweep — with the Harbor `:44831` ticker seat as battery home. `[INLINE-CRATE]`

**Step (a) — seed the roster.** Two rosters enter; only one leaves.

- `R_mem` = the remembered roster (the last sweep's live seats — "the remembered-three"): what the serving layer *believes* is hot.
- `P` = the fresh probe stream: per-seat probes executed now, each **responder-verified** (SELD read-side evidence-plane class): the seat's own responder must answer with the ticker payload shape — a proxy echo, a cached page, or a third party does not count as life. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`
- Classification per seat: `200-fresh / 429-throttled / 404-gone / timeout`.
- Drift sets: `D = R_mem \ fresh-live` (remembered but not live now — the stale set) and `N = fresh-live \ R_mem` (newly live — the surprise set).

**Step (b) — run the serving adaptation over one cadence.** With the admitted set = fresh-live seats sorted by `rho` (§1.3), apply the adapted split `θ` (§1.2), execute the three-state grid (§1.4d) on each probe outcome, and emit probe receipts + landing receipts as distinct records (§1.4b). This is one corner of the MAPE-style design space `(θ, h, admission-threshold)`, chosen to stress the seam where memory meets evidence: the stale-roster seam. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

**Step (c) — measured criteria.**

- **C1 hot-first:** every fresh-live seat appears earlier in the admission order than any rested seat (checkable from the rho-sorted log alone).
- **C2 cold-rested:** every 429/404/decayed seat is rested or re-pinned — none assumed live, none served on a stale receipt.
- **C3 dispositions receipted:** every three-state disposition carries `{state, seat_id, probe_id, ts}`; no unmetered action.
- **C4 stage separation:** probe receipts and landing receipts are distinct record types; no merged or inherited validity anywhere in the run log.

**Step (d) — the FLAG condition (the falsifier's bite).** The probe is **FLAGGED** if either:

- **F1 stale-roster admission:** any seat admitted from `R_mem` whose fresh probe failed (`D` nonempty at admission time) — the serving trusted the remembered roster over fresh evidence.
- **F2 decay-gated re-pin:** any decayed receipt still gates a re-pin — a seat re-pinned on a receipt older than `h` without a same-cadence fresh probe.

On FLAG: the offending seat ids, their receipts, and the drift set `D` are written into this finding; the run does not silently pass. The meters' run record stays `[ABSENT]-with-flag` until the fold's execution lands either a clean run or a flagged-but-receipted run — both are admissible results; an unlogged run is not. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

### 2.2 Why this falsifies

The target mechanism's whole claim is that agent-adaptive serving beats the non-adaptive baseline *because* it tracks the access pattern instead of a remembered list. F1/F2 are precisely the failure modes of tracking-the-list-instead-of-the-pattern. A design that passes C1–C4 while never triggering F1/F2 has demonstrated the mechanism's core seam under the rig's own surfaces; a design that triggers them has located the exact line where the adaptation leaks. Either outcome is a result — silence is not. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

### 2.3 Battery discipline

The sweep never outruns the seat's throttle: probes respect retry-after, 429s are treated as evidence (throttled seats rest), and the cadence `C` is bounded below by the ticker's publish interval. The probe is a guest on `:44831`, not a flooder. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (battery home named); `[DESIGN-NOTE]` (discipline terms)

## 3. THE ROTATION TERM

### 3.1 Re-probe every cadence — the roster is never remembered

The rotation-watch sweeps the **entire** roster each cadence `C`; there is no skip, no "trusted since last time", no carry-over. Returned seats re-slot immediately into the rho-sorted admission order — the order is rebuilt from fresh receipts every sweep, so no seat's position survives from a previous cadence ("never the remembered-three"). `[INLINE-CRATE]` (rotation-watch named as the proxy surface); `[DESIGN-NOTE]` (immediate re-slot semantics)

### 3.2 Decay schedule — the liveness receipt's half-life

A receipt's validity decays with half-life `h`. Default `h = C` — one cadence — so a receipt is exactly as old as the longest it could have gone un-refreshed; anything older is inert by construction. The schedule breathes with an AIMD discipline (Reward-Driven Collider adaptive-envelope class):

- **No FLAG events across a window** → additive increase: `h += δ` (slow open), bounded above at `h_max = 4C`.
- **Any FLAG event (F1 or F2)** → multiplicative decrease: `h := max(h_min, h/2)` (fast close), floored at `h_min = C/2`.

`h` is a receipt parameter recorded on every receipt; it is never a runtime vibe and never tuned mid-cadence. `[INLINE-CRATE]` (AIMD bearing); `[DESIGN-NOTE]` (δ, bounds, trigger wiring)

### 3.3 Rotation invariants (violations are themselves FLAG events)

1. Sweep covers 100% of the roster — no seat skipped, no partial sweep passed off as full.
2. Every returned seat re-slots within the same cadence — drift `D`/`N` reflected in the order before service begins.
3. No seat's order position survives a failed probe — a seat that fails drops out of the order immediately, whatever it was yesterday.
4. Decayed receipts are inert — they gate nothing: not admission, not re-pin, not rho beyond the maximal-`H` tax.

Any breach of 1–4 during the run is logged as its own FLAG (class: rotation-invariant violation), independent of F1/F2. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

## 4. THE METERS

All meters read only receipts (probe + landing) — the evidence plane, never seat internals. Targets are stated so execution has a pass/fail; nothing here is measured until the fold's execution lands. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

| meter | definition | target |
|---|---|---|
| **roster-admission-precision** | live-admitted / all-admitted (live = fresh probe passed at admission time) | 1.0 — any gap is an F1 smell |
| **stale-admission-rate** | decayed-admitted / all-admitted | 0 — the FLAG's fuel; any non-zero triggers F1 review |
| **re-pin-latency** | decayed seats re-pinned within one cadence / decayed seats | ≥ 0.9; a seat that cannot re-pin within C escalates to NEEDS-THE-CHAIR |
| **three-state compliance** | dispositions landed with receipts / dispositions issued | 1.0 — an unreceipted disposition is an unmetered action |
| **run record** | the clean/flagged receipted run itself | `[ABSENT]` unless the fold's execution lands |

Each meter is computed over exactly one cadence `C` (the sweep) so the numbers are attributable to a single rotation term; multi-cadence aggregation happens only after per-cadence records exist. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

The meters deliberately avoid TTFT/P95 as primary measures — not because they don't matter (they are the field's headline), but because this lane cannot read live latency (no reads). Admission precision and stale-admission-rate are the falsifier's native meters; latency is delegated to the execution fold that owns the live surface. `[ABSENT]` for live TTFT/P95 here; `[INLINE-CRATE]` for their status in the field class.

### 5.2 The two-way door

The design is reversible; nothing here burns a bridge. If the FLAG persists after execution — stale-roster admissions (F1) or decay-gated re-pins (F2) observed in the landed run — then this design note stands as written and the alternatives are named, not forced:

- **alt-1 — harden the probe-at-fire mandate:** make same-cadence-probe-less admission impossible *by construction*: extend compile-time gating so the admission order can only be built from receipts stamped in the current cadence. Memory becomes telemetry, never eligibility. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`
- **alt-2 — shorten the half-life:** set `h := C/2` permanently and lower `h_min` to `C/4`; decay outruns staleness by a factor of two. Costs: more probes per unit time, more 429 exposure at the battery seat. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`
- **alt-3 — retire the remembered roster:** every cadence seeds purely from the fresh probe; `R_mem` is demoted to a displayed artifact with zero gating power. Simplest, most honest, least adaptive — it throws away the pattern-tracking that justifies the mechanism, so it is the last resort, not the first. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`

The chair picks among stand-as-designed / alt-1 / alt-2 / alt-3, or orders the probe re-run. Never forced: a flagged design that is adopted without receipted deliberation would violate the very stage-decoupling this probe exists to test. `[DESIGN-NOTE]`
