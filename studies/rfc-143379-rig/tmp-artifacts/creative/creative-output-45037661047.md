## The K-recency gate: certified FAIL, then two-branch science

The K-recency hard window failed the RFC's own gate at every horizon of 300 cycles and above. The demand-age instrument said FAIL: worst-case minimum minus 20.4 at four times, minus 30 at two times, sub-zero in all 25 seeds at four times and 300 cycles and beyond. The means eroded from plus 23.9 to plus 0.6. The K-scaling arm said no: a sub-linearly scaled window underperformed the fixed ten-window at every reported ratio. That was branch one — the gate's letter, failed, with the mechanism named.

Branch two was the science that resolved the fail to a spec. The constraint arm: recency-truncation is the worst strategy, at 1.00, against hierarchical at 0.25, against pinned restores at 0. The aliasing-times-proximity arm: confirmed both, R-squared 0.655 against a bar of 0.35. The competition arm: not confirmed — an instrument limit, not a mechanism's absence, because the rivals never faced the window-hit path. The archive arm: FAIL, then refuted under the tested construction — ID-known recall is the optimistic bound, and the budget knob admits only the demanded literal's ID. The pinning arm: protects — recurrence-aware exact-literal pinning drops the deep-minima prevalence from 1.00 to 0.15. The portability arm: a gap, then recurred — pinning protects only where literals recur; real git-log streams carry recurrence of 0.284. The retention arm, the first invention: retention breaks the cell — full retention drops the death cell from 1.00 to 0.00, at a cost of three reserved literals, three tokens. The compaction seam: confirmed — 17 of 21 lost facts, 81 percent, are tiny and old. The same geometry is the pinning's death cell and the compaction front's loss cell. The tiny-aged fact is the system's universal fragility point.

The spec that followed: a window that must grow faster than the aging curve — the two-N window — asked to outrun the demand's age. And the counters: exact-literal pinning at 18 tokens against the field's 47, under half a percent at production scale; and the retention latch, all or nothing, not a dial.
