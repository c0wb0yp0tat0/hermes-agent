The night did not open with a triumph. It opened with a clock that had drifted, and a line that said FAIL, and a decision made before the first rung: nothing would be re-seeded from what had already landed. Everything still standing would come fresh from the remainder. That is where the arc starts — not at the top of a hill, but at a re-anchored clock, with the finish line held honest until the operator's word arrived.

## The re-anchored clock

A pre-launch fan found the standing state had to be re-staged. Restore the read path. Network. Governance. Compaction. Four frames anchored in a row: the pull request body read end to end, the lens-artifacts folded into the spine, the pilot start-line set on the read-restore's own pass, and the fleet riding free-only, every apex role on the free flagship. The thread's machine-log became the running record. The gate held: nothing public without the operator's word. The ship was free, the clock was reset, and the night began.

## Fire, measure, land, fold, fire

The arc's shape, in the record's own voice, is five words: fire, measure, land, fold, fire. Every phase's receipts became the next phase's seed. Nothing untracked, nothing un-receipted. The measurement phase was the honest arm — receipts, harness, drift-registers. The landing phase produced verdicts. The fold composed the separated legs into one state. And the fold re-armed the next fire on the composed state. That loop — arm, ground, run, fold, verify, re-arm — is the whole method, and it ran all night.

## The composition's falsification

The center of the night was Track One: the composed state of three pull requests — network, read-restore, governance — falsified against each other. ## The write-gate's rebase landing

## The K-recency gate, certified FAIL

Then the keystone gate was run on its own terms, and it did not pass. Not at three hundred cycles. Not at any horizon tested. The promise that a hard recency window would hold knowledge alive failed, and it failed seed-universally, with deep sub-zero excursions that did not wash out with more data. Worst-case minimums dropped to minus twenty point four at four times, minus thirty at two times; sub-zero in all twenty-five seeds at four times and three hundred cycles and beyond. Means eroded fourfold, from plus twenty-three point nine to plus zero point six. The K-scaling arm said no: a sub-linearly scaled window underperformed the fixed ten-window at every reported ratio.

This is the part of the night that mattered most, because the machinery did exactly what an honest tool should do: it reported its own bad result instead of being talked into a good one. A certified fail is not the end of the argument. It is the terms of the next argument.

## The two-branch science

Faced with that fail, the work did not collapse to one verdict. It split into two branches on purpose, and both were kept open.

The first branch: the gate as proposed is not a gate. Hardness comes from elsewhere, and the probe's repairs belong to the probe, not to the bar. The read-side promise has to be re-derived from measurement, not asserted. Constraint-pinning works where recency-truncation is the worst strategy — one point zero zero against pinned restores at zero. Aliasing times proximity is confirmed both, with a predictor clearing its bar with room to spare. Competition is not confirmed — an instrument limit, not a mechanism's absence, because rivals never faced the window-hit path. Archive fails, then refutes under the tested construction: ID-known recall is the optimistic bound, and the budget knob admits only the demanded literal's ID. Pinning protects — recurrence-aware exact-literal pinning drops deep-minima prevalence from one point zero zero to zero point one five — but only where literals recur; real git-log streams carry recurrence of zero point two eight four. Retention breaks the cell: full retention drops the death cell from one point zero zero to zero point zero zero, at a cost of three reserved literals, three tokens. The compaction seam confirms it: seventeen of twenty-one lost facts, eighty-one percent, are tiny and old. The same geometry is the pinning's death cell and the compaction front's loss cell. The tiny-aged fact is the system's universal fragility point.

The second branch: the gate survives, but only if the window runs wide enough to outrun the aging curve. The two-N window. A memory's real age is measured not from when it was written but from when it was last needed, and need ages at an accelerating rate. A window that merely covers the mean leaves the tail to rot; the window must outrun the curve, which means it scales with the function, not with a convenient constant.

Both branches stand. Neither is chosen yet. That openness is not indecision — it is the method.

## The 2N window

The two-N window is the afternoon's sharpest image. If aging is a curve and not a line, then any fixed count of recent items is a bet that the curve stays flat. It does not. The window must grow with the thing it is protecting, otherwise the oldest pinned facts — the exact literals, the constraints that may never drift — fall out the back while everyone is watching the front. The window is a promise that the thing being protected gets to define its own size. And the counters ride beside it: exact-literal pinning at eighteen tokens against the field's forty-seven, under half a percent at production scale; and the retention latch, all or nothing, not a dial.

## The harness's four self-repairs

The harness repaired itself four times through the night, and each repair was named by the seam it closed.

The first seam was composition: merge or abort, never skip, because a skipped conflict is a lie dressed as progress. The second was the test battery: one composed state, one honest battery, run where it lives, not where it is convenient. The third was the claim check: each thread's promises re-verified on the shared branch, so no one gets to say "it worked before." The fourth was the receipt: append-only rows, need to task to evidence, exit codes and artifact paths, so the morning can read the night without guessing.

Four seams, four repairs, all of them local, all of them committed where the branch could see them. The read-restore fix sat underneath them all: read snapshots the document without the writer's file lock, so a paused read can resurrect stale state after a newer commit. Seventeen passing tests. The reverted store failing the regression.

## The fleet's refires

The fleet re-fired more than once, and the reason was the same each time: a higher claim cannot stand on a lower claim that has not been proven. Once the read restore carried its real fix and its proof upstream, the things gated on it were allowed to move. Until then they waited. Waiting, here, is not delay; it is dependency living up to its name.

The fabric dispatched twice. Fabric one point zero, universal pin, thirty-one rows, thirty of thirty-one red on the checkout class — the requested-equals-executed assert. That red became the mechanism decision: re-dispatch at per-fork pins. Fabric two point zero, thirty rows, thirty of thirty green at the per-fork pins, plus the subject fork's own green. The matrix ran its dual surface — both legs green, same pinned head, fail-fast off. The thread carried two hundred eighty-two rows. The box held at ninety-one percent under guard, six point one gigabytes free. The refires were not a sign that anything had been broken; they were a sign that the order of proof was being respected.

## The field's corroborations arriving independently

None of this was done in a vacuum. Corroboration came from outside, on its own schedule, and it agreed with the inside on the hard parts. Governance decay, quoted back at us: recency is neither a reliable proxy for relevance nor a sufficient mechanism — the study's central fail, in the field's own words. The Compaction Cliff: a rule and a log compete for the same tokens; only the rule needs exact wording, fifty-three percent falling to ten across five rounds. Knowledge Triage's TypeRetrieve: in-scope rules pinned ahead of relevance, one hundred percent recall at fifty against seventy-three. ARC: an ID-addressable append-only store plus bounded active view plus recall chunks makes every stored observation exactly recoverable — ninety-nine point four percent against eighty-eight point one. The competition class, measured across one hundred twenty-six thousand four hundred and seventy-three trials: contexts without a conflict lose two hundredths to thirty-four hundredths of a nat and stay ninety-seven to one hundred percent accurate; with one conflict, one point three eight to three point two four nats and twenty-five to fifty-two percent. The instrument's universal sub-zero excursions were never a noise floor. They were the unmodeled competition arm. The field named what the study had measured, and the study had measured what the field named. Neither was derived from the other. They did not coordinate. They converged. That is the strongest form of evidence there is: two rooms, same result, no shared notes.
