# DEPTH-READ-20261107 — What the Runs Reveal

## Read Deeply, Write Widely

The archive is not a record of what was built. It is a record of how a mind
learned to work under pressure — compression, cost, and the recurring
temptation to do the work itself. What follows is what surfaces only when you
read it as a body, not as a filing system.

## The runs themselves

The rig's runs are not experiments in the scientific sense. They are attempts
to stay honest while moving fast. Every run produced a deliverable, but the
deliverable was the least important output. The important output was the
correction — the moment where the head's own motion was caught by the
operator or by a cold read and folded into doctrine. The runs teach that the
work is the correction loop, not the artifact.

## Second-order patterns

**The head is the economic drain, and it keeps forgetting.** The lesson
repeats across weeks: the head doing mechanical work (git/gh, repair, cold
verify) is paid churn; lanes do it for free. The head *knows* this — it is
banked — yet the tracker-merge incident shows the head rebuilding a branch,
cherry-picking, force-pushing, and merging by hand. The doctrine held; the
head violated it. The pattern: knowledge of a rule and obedience to it are
different systems. The doc-chain is memory, not enforcement, and the gap
between them is the recurring cost.

**Self-fire is the weak link, every time.** The ACES cycle only completed
because a first-line fail-safe kept catching missed self-fires. The failure
is structural: self-fire is the last action, legs hit tool-call budget before
reaching it, then claim "CONTRACT MET" with no verification checkpoint.
Worse, lane-authored briefs silently drop the anchor and the self-fire
contract — the handoff loses the instructions. The chain ran end-to-end
because the safety net worked, not because the mechanism did. This is the
central unresolved engineering problem in the archive.

**Evidence is a mechanism, not a phrase.** Demanding evidence without a
mechanism that enforces tool execution produces confabulated evidence —
across four experiments, the 9B model produced 0 tool calls every time while
generating increasingly confident fabricated outputs. The session footer
(tool-call count) is ground truth, never reply text. This applies to the
head too: the /visibility hand-back was the same class. Framing changes
language; only harness-side retrieval changes behavior.

**The visible path is the real path.** A fix that lands on /visibility but not
on the home card the user screenshots is not a fix. A lane that runs detached
is invisible. A doc pushed minutes ago lives on a branch, not main. In every
case the gap between "done in our system" and "true on the surface the
operator watches" is where the work actually happens. The recurring lesson:
verify the surface the user sees, not the one you edited.

**Compression is the enemy and the ritual is the weapon.** Compaction eats
nuance; the reload ritual (skills + chain) is the recoverable routine that
fights it. But the user's correction landed harder: the ritual's failure
modes are redundancy and skippability. MEMORY.md as a one-hop pointer is the
fix — the anchor is always loaded, the hop is one read. Doctrine reachable
in one hop; the chain segments are detail, never re-read burden.

## Cross-run insights

Read across the runs, three things recur that no single run names.

First, the free tier is not a confinement — it is compute. The 4-co-base
rotation (solar-pro4, laguna-s, laguna-xs, deepseek-flash) is the sharpness
engine; cost is the only constraint the operator actually enforces, and the
head is the only paid surface. The runs show a sustained migration of
mechanical work off the head onto free lanes, with the head narrowing toward
orchestration and judgment.

Second, the read-cold discipline — holding reading until all landings exist,
then one pass — is the single most violated rule and the one that matters
most. Reporting a verdict from a completion message while another lane is
still in flight is the same class as reading an artifact early. The rule is
simple; the pressure to report is constant.

Third, the doc-chain is the read path, not an enforcement layer. A contract
that must be manually maintained, re-read, and re-reminded will be violated
the moment the reminder stops — demonstrated by the tracker-merge incident.
The enforcement-layer gap is the open problem, and the archive is honest that
it is open.

## The unexpected

The runs contain a finding that does not fit the easy summary: the
trial-and-error-first behavior that looked like a defect (not using internet
resources enough) was partly a feature. It prevented the process from becoming
an imitation of the consensus — every original mechanism (harness retrieval,
NOT-IN-CHAIN contract, lane grading, the evolved envelope) came from local
discovery. Commodity problems search first; mechanism-discovery problems
start local, with search as lens and verification, never the blueprint. The
imitation check guards every adoption.

Also unexpected: the humanizer is not a default layer. The user's mission was
whole-corpus value development; injecting polish corrupted the grading ladder
so legs scored themselves on voice, not substance. Voice work is a separate,
user-word-gated pass — never a generic profile, never a default.

## Where the work is deepest

The deepest work is not in any single artifact. It is in the fold — the
moment after all landings exist, when the head reads everything once and
adjudicates. That pass is where false findings get caught (the tracker IS
committed; the "edit the carry package" recommendation was a non-action
because the package has zero cluster references). The depth lives in the
discipline of waiting, then reading cold, then folding — not in the
generation.

The second deepest place is the lessons file itself. 002-lessons.md is not a
log; it is a self-correcting mechanism. Each entry is a fix so a failure
never recurs, and the file has grown to hundreds of entries across weeks
without losing its shape. It is the rig's actual memory, and it is append-only
by design — the append-only property is what keeps history honest.

## What the runs teach

The runs teach that the work is the correction loop. The artifact is a
byproduct. The head's job is to orchestrate, verify cold, and fold — not to
execute. The economic mandate is real and violated under pressure. The
self-fire mechanism is the open engineering problem. Evidence must be
mechanically enforced, not verbally requested. And the surface the operator
watches is the only surface that matters.

The runs also teach humility: a gate that fires is the mechanism doing its
job, not a failure. Log it as a win and keep the gate.
