# THE SELF-PERPETUATING LOOP — the architecture (the refactor-type move, 2026-11-06)

**Purpose:** the loop that runs itself — every ring's lanes ground on the accumulated record, the record grows with every landing, and the next ring reads the grown thing. The morning's refactor-momentum, formalized as the rig's operating cycle.

## The cycle (one ring)
1. **ARM** — the ring's nodes selected by VOI from the record's standing candidates (each node: its acceptance-block, its seed, its base). The finish-line for the ring is written FIRST (the failable check that can fail).
2. **GROUND** — every starting-lane reads PR #1's thread end-to-end (the complete chronology — the contract, the receipts, the verdicts, the experiments, the machine-log), THEN its seed. No lane starts cold into the vacuum; every lane starts warm on the whole.
3. **RUN** — the ready-nodes dispatch in parallel (the fleet's seats, the runners' windows, the board's lanes), each under its frozen brief with the think-tank's spine and the LP-armament (the envelope, the statuses, the wall clause).
4. **FOLD** — every landing posts to the thread on arrival (the cadence, held); the ledger takes its row; the classes become guards, the guards become commits, the findings become the record's next ground.
5. **VERIFY** — the finish-line re-runs at the last landing; two consecutive PASSes with zero new classes is the no-improvement proof; a third run confirms it.
6. **RE-ARM** — the next ring's nodes come from the record's grown candidates; the loop perpetuates itself through the one place.

## The invariants (binding)
- The thread is the ground and the record: read end-to-end before work, post on landing, never write elsewhere publicly.
- The anchor's untouched by curation; the chain's the unbounded home; the fold routes downstream.
- The quiet-boundaries hold: the RFC's issue quiet until the word; the owners' scopes theirs; the rig rides publicly nowhere but the PR.
- The disciplines ride every ring: failures are findings; the proud hour is the careless hour; the long-designed run is the LET; the no-flattening's depth is the standard.
- The stewards' frame: the runner's unmetered and governance-kept; the disk's guarded; the receipts are the trust.

## The refactor-type's signature (why this is the morning's momentum)
The morning's run built the structure in big strokes; this architecture IS the structure's next stroke — the loop's full shape, written once, wielded by every ring after. The consolidation-tempo's products (the guards, the classes, the measured slope) become the architecture's ground, and the creation-tempo returns as the loop's own engine.
