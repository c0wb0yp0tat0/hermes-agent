# LOSS-LADDER-20261121 — the transparency-compact study on OUR seams

The field's transparency-compact study planted facts by source-type and measured
retention after compaction per type. We replicated on the rig's own seams —
the TWEAK/NATIVE battery class (deterministic, zero-billed, additive) — at three
depths of the seat's window (0.5 / 0.65 / 0.8) and scored by the exact-match
grader after the compression pass.

## Retention table (per source-type × depth; 10 episodes per cell)

| source-type | depth 0.5 | depth 0.65 | depth 0.8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| user-stated | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| assistant-prose | 0.000 | 0.000 | 1.000 |
| tool-output | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| reasoning-only | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |

## The verdict: which depth keeps the load-bearing classes intact

Load-bearing classes = hard-lines (user-stated) + anchors' markers
(assistant-prose) + receipts (tool-output).

- **0.5** and **0.65** both collapse every non-head class to 0.000: they retain
  only the user-stated head. The 0.65 band rides on head-only retention; it does
  NOT keep the anchors' markers or the receipts.
- **0.8** is the minimum depth that restores the **anchors' markers**
  (assistant-prose → 1.000). It keeps the user-stated hard-lines too.
- **The receipts (tool-output) stay 0.000 at every depth** — the compressor
  prunes the middle (tool-output) regardless of depth.
- **The reasoning-only anchors stay 0.000 at every depth** — the reasoning trace
  is excluded from the concised window, so a reason-only fact can never ride the
  window the reader sees.

**No depth keeps ALL load-bearing classes intact.** The minimum depth that keeps
the anchors' markers is **0.8**; the 0.65 band is NOT load-bearing for the
anchors' markers or the receipts. The evidence the 0.65 band rides is the
user-stated head only (1.000 at 0.5 and 0.65), and that is precisely the finding
the lane's transparency goal captures: at 0.65 the window is still a head, not a
representative window.

## The evidence

Ledger `probes/LOSS-LADDER-20261121.json` (generated by
`probes/loss_ladder.py`, the deterministic TWEAK/NATIVE battery class):

- `verified`: the retention matrix above (10 episodes per cell).
- `matrix`: per-episode retained flags (30 booleans per source-type).
- `decision_rule`: pre-committed compose — `load_bearing`, `all_lb_intact_0.65`,
  `min_depth_keep_load_bearing`, `anchor_markers_restored_at_0.8`.
- `per_episode`: the one-run rows (episode, four facts, retained per depth).

The depth math (mirrors the rig's `ContextCompressor`): at depth `d` the pass
retains the outermost `d` fraction of the window (head + tail) and compacts the
middle. So 0.5 keeps the outer 25% of the window, 0.65 the outer 32.5%, 0.8 the
outer 40%.
