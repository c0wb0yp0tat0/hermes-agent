Source: https://pub.towardsai.net/what-is-agi-and-what-is-a-harness-explained-simply-7d21655fc68f
(Towards AI, Kamrun Nahar, Sep 2026. VERIFIED OPENING: the Tahoe anecdote — three lines of
typing talked a dealership chatbot into "selling" an 0k SUV for ; the bot had no idea what
it had agreed to. AGI defined: one system that can do any thinking job a person can, including

CORE (head-distilled, honest): the model ≠ the system. The harness — context, tools, gates,
guardrails, the channel — decides what a model's capability becomes in the world. The Tahoe
bot's failure was a harness failure (no price-taking guardrail), not a model failure. "AGI" is
the shimmer; the harness is the artifact you actually build, ship, and measure.
TRANSFER HOOKS: (1) the study's instruments are ALL harness-level (the read action is a harness
change; the K-window is a channel change; phase-0 is harness doctrine — "environment first").
(2) Instrument candidate: the harness-delta ledger — classify every change MODEL / HARNESS /
CHANNEL (the trinity's three layers), and the pilot gate measures HARNESS deltas by the
same-model-two-harnesses AB (the read flip: the SAME model, one revert — the RFC's experimental
shape, standardized). (3) The Tahoe class: a guardrail with no price-taking — the study's own
version: a read with no lock (the spec-2/мsvcrt questions) — the gate IS the guardrail.
