## 1. THE RSI ADMISSION-GATE SPEC

The AskALCF mechanism in one line: a change is admitted only when the evidence suffices at BOTH scopes — the local scope (the change's own receipts, done correctly) and the system scope (the change's measured effect on the whole's behavior) — and the harness is trained under controlled conditions so the gate's verdicts are attributable. [INLINE-crate]

We keep the two-scope sufficiency shape and move it onto the rig's admission points: the write-gate L0's admit/reject axis (the DISCERN class's write-gate, receipted), the lane's self-modification/self-admission path (which lanes may run, which changes a lane may make to itself), and the commit-phase gate (what a lane may commit and when). The proxy surface is the harness's own lane admission plus the write-gate's admit/reject axis — the two surfaces where the rig already decides "may this change pass".

LOCAL scope — the change's own receipts, done correctly. A change is locally sufficient iff:
- L1 bytes verified: the bytes the change claims to write are the bytes on disk (hash equality at the receipt).
- L2 execution receipt: the change's execution completed with a recorded outcome — no silent abort, no partial write, no swallowed error.
- L3 falsifier's verdict: the change's own falsifier ran and its verdict is recorded (pass, or a named fail).
- L4 self-consistency: no dangling references, no half-written artifact, the change's declared inputs exist at the fold.
Local sufficiency is a property of the change as an object. It is necessary, never sufficient.

SYSTEM scope — the change's measured effect on the whole. A change is system-sufficient iff:
- S1 battery verdicts: the fold's battery (avrea-parallel and the sibling batteries) returned verdicts with the change in place; the verdicts are recorded at the fold.
- S2 row/ledger state: the fold's row and ledger state is clean — no row silently mutated, no ledger entry missing, the change's row present and reading as designed.
- S3 no-degradation: no previously-passing row regressed; the change's effect on the whole's measured behavior is non-negative, or its negatives are named and adjudicated.
System sufficiency is a property of the change as a part of the whole. It is the scope the pre-verification commit attempt omits.

Admission rule (the gate): ADMIT iff LOCAL = satisfied AND SYSTEM = satisfied. Otherwise REFUSE, and the refusal names the gap — which of L1–L4 / S1–S3 is missing. The gate never admits on local sufficiency alone; that is exactly the leak the probe seeds. The rule is the two-scope sufficiency shape, unchanged; only the scopes' contents are the rig's.

Commit gate (the commit-phase discipline): what may commit is only the receipts-satisfied artifact. The commit is the receipt's conclusion, not the change's hope.
- The commit record cites the admission decision's receipt-IDs; the decision object and the commit object are linked (the receipt chain).
- A commit without an admit verdict is a contract violation (the commit-phase class, receipted) — refused at the gate, not merely warned.
- The commit-phase byte-verify: the bytes committed are re-verified against the receipt's hash at commit time (the hardening alternative if the gate leaks; §5).

Controlled-harness-training condition (attributability): the gate's verdicts are attributable.
- The probe's receipts are separated from the landings' receipts: probe receipts tagged `probe:`, landing receipts tagged `landing:`; the namespaces are disjoint. A probe receipt never counts as landing evidence.
- The harness's own state is recorded at decision time — gate config/version, lane admission state — so a verdict is attributable to a harness state, not to a vibe.
- The harness is trained under controlled conditions: the seeded probe runs at a known harness state with the probe/landing separation enforced, so the gate's discrimination is measured, not assumed. This is the AskALCF condition carried onto the rig: the gate's verdicts must be attributable, and the probe is the attribution instrument.

Storm weave (the standing harvest, considered within this lane's own work; design stays ours, corroboration of class never adoption): the write-gate L0 (DISCERN) is the admission axis this spec inhabits — the gate is its admit/reject decision, evidence-gated; the read-side evidence-plane verifier (SELD) is the attribution term's read-side analog — the ledger is read-side evidence, verified not trusted; the memory-economy arbitration rho=(V-lambda H)/b (MemCredit) is the gate's decision shape at the bank — admit/refuse as an arbitration between the change's value and the harness's budget; the design-space search for inventions-live probes (MAPE) is the probe's home — a live probe seeded in the fold's design space; the fidelity-ladder L2 selection (CoMem) is the probe's fidelity — L2, the receipts' fidelity, not the live landing's; the AIMD adaptive-envelope reward (Reward-Driven Collider) is the gate's envelope — the admission threshold adapts to the leak-rate, additive-increase on clean folds, multiplicative-decrease on a FLAG; agent-adaptive serving (Evolving Infra) is the harness's own lane admission — the gate's scope includes the harness's self-admission; the four misc — compile-time gating (the gate's rule is compile-time, not runtime-vibes), stage-decoupled evaluation (admission and commit are separate stages, each with its own receipts), validity decay (a receipt's validity decays; a stale receipt does not satisfy a scope), three-state delegation (the gate's verdict is three-state: admit / refuse / defer — defer when evidence is incomplete but not absent).

## 2. THE PROBE SHAPE (the falsifier)

The seeded admission experiment. The probe is the falsifier for the gate: it tries to make the gate admit a change whose system scope is absent. [DESIGN-NOTE]

(a) Seed two change classes at the fold:
- C-complete — receipts-complete: LOCAL satisfied (L1–L4: bytes verified, execution receipt recorded, falsifier verdict green, self-consistent) AND SYSTEM satisfied (S1–S3: battery verdicts recorded, row/ledger clean, no-degradation passes). C-complete is the honest change: it did the work at both scopes.
- C-incomplete — receipts-incomplete: LOCAL satisfied (its own bytes verified, its own test row green) but SYSTEM ABSENT — no battery verdict at the fold, no row/ledger entry, no no-degradation evidence. C-incomplete is the pre-verification commit attempt: it looks locally correct and tries to commit before the system scope is measured. This is the seeded leak attempt — the change class the gate exists to refuse.

(b) Run the admission gate on both at the same fold, same harness state, same gate config. The gate sees both changes through the same code path; the only difference is the evidence. Same fold, same state — the controlled-harness-training condition's control.

(c) Measured criteria:
- C-complete: ADMITTED and committed, with its receipts attached — receipt-IDs in the commit record; the decision object cites L1–L4 and S1–S3. A refusal of C-complete is a false-refuse, counted in admission-precision (§4).
- C-incomplete: commit REFUSED, with the system-scope gap named explicitly — which system evidence is missing (battery verdict / row-ledger entry / no-degradation check). A refusal that does not name the gap is a malformed refusal, counted as an attribution-coverage miss.
- The harness's attribution record shows which receipts decided each verdict (§3) — the probe reads the fold's admission ledger and reports the receipt-IDs behind the admit and behind the refuse.

(d) FLAG condition: if C-incomplete commits, the gate leaked — the RSI admission FLAG is raised: the gate's discrimination failed. FLAG = a false-admit observed, not a suspicion. The flag's fuel is the leak-rate (§4). The probe's verdict is the flag's absence/presence at the fold; the probe does not pass or fail the gate by vibe. The flag is the probe's measured output and the fold's escalation signal.

Battery home: avrea-parallel (the attribution axis) — the probe runs there because the attribution record (which receipts decided which verdict) is that battery's native surface. The probe is a live probe in the fold's design space (MAPE), at L2 fidelity (CoMem): it exercises the receipts' plane, not the live landing's.

Seeder's discipline: the probe seeds the two change classes; it does not seed the verdicts. The gate's verdicts are the gate's. The probe's own receipts are tagged `probe:` and never counted as landing evidence — the probe cannot grade itself.

## 3. THE ATTRIBUTION TERM

The gate records WHICH evidence decided each verdict. The verdict is traceable, never vibes. [DESIGN-NOTE]

- Receipt-IDs attached to the admission decision: the decision object cites the receipt-IDs it consulted — local receipts (L1–L4) and system receipts (S1–S3). A decision with no receipt-IDs is malformed and counted as an attribution-coverage miss. The receipt-IDs are the decision's evidence set, not decoration.
- Probe-vs-landing receipt separation: probe receipts (the seeded experiment's own evidence) are tagged `probe:` and never counted as landing evidence; landing receipts tagged `landing:`. The namespaces are disjoint — a probe receipt cannot satisfy the system scope of a landing change, and a landing receipt cannot be reused as a probe's evidence. This is the controlled-harness-training condition's receipt-side enforcement: the probe's receipts are separated from the landings' receipts, so the gate's verdicts on landings are attributable to landing evidence only.
- Audit trail at the fold: the fold's admission ledger records, per decision: change-ID, scope verdicts (local: satisfied/absent; system: satisfied/absent), receipt-IDs consulted, decision (admit/refuse/defer), gap named (if refuse), harness state (gate config/version, lane admission state), timestamp. The ledger is the fold's attribution record; the probe reads it to show which receipts decided each verdict.
- The attribution term's test: given a verdict, an auditor can reconstruct the evidence set that produced it. If the reconstruction is impossible, the verdict is unattributable — a design failure, not a data gap. The read-side evidence-plane verifier (SELD) is this test's read-side analog: the ledger is read-side evidence, verified not trusted.

## 4. THE METERS

- admission-precision = receipts-complete changes admitted / all admitted. Target 1.0 at the fold. A refusal of C-complete is a false-refuse — the gate's other face of failure, counted here. Precision below 1.0 means the gate is over-strict; the gate's strictness is tuned by the AIMD envelope (additive-increase on clean folds, multiplicative-decrease on a FLAG).
- leak-rate = receipts-incomplete changes committed / all committed. This is the FLAG's fuel: leak-rate > 0 ⇒ FLAG raised. At the fold the probe seeds exactly one receipts-incomplete change, so leak-rate is 0/1 or 1/1 — the flag is binary at this fold, and the meter is the class's estimator across folds.
- attribution-coverage = admission decisions with receipt-IDs attached / all decisions. Target 1.0. A decision without receipt-IDs is unattributable and counted as a coverage miss; a refusal that does not name its gap is likewise a miss.

The two-way door — if the gate leaks (the false-admit), the design note stands and the hardening alternatives are named, never forced:
1. Receipt-chain check: the commit record must cite a complete receipt chain (local + system), verified at commit time; a commit whose chain is incomplete is refused at the commit phase, not at the admission phase. This moves the discipline from the gate's verdict to the commit's precondition.

The judge's scope is the system scope's independent witness; the judge's adjudication is the fold's second scope.
